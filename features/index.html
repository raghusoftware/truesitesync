One entry on site, used by every module after it

The reason to keep it on one platform: nobody types the same thing twice. Here's the path a single day on site takes.

1
Site records the day

Attendance, material issued, machine hours and the day sheet — entered on the mobile app, offline if needed.

2
Work is verified

Supervisors and engineers confirm quantities, close snags and approve what actually got done.

3
Cost lands on the project

Material, labour and equipment hours become project cost automatically — no re-entry.

4
Billing & accounts settle

5
