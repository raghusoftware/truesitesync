# Market Trends: Schemes, Budgets and Market Events Driving Rising Contractor/Builder Search Demand in India (2025–Oct 2026)

Research method note: the WebSearch tool is US-located and returns summarised results; WebFetch failed with DNS errors (theweek.in, prsindia.org), so full-text verification of several articles was not possible. No keyword-volume tool (Google Trends, Ahrefs, GSC) was available, so "rising interest" is inferred from news volume, dated official releases, and the appearance of explainer/consultant content, not from measured search volume. Several hits are aggregators, exam-prep sites (Vision IAS, Anantam IAS) or commercial blogs, which are flagged where used.

## Q1. Union Budget 2026-27 capex and new programmes; PMAY-U 2.0 / PMAY-G; Jal Jeevan Mission; urban schemes

### Takeaway
Budget 2026-27 (1 Feb 2026) raised public capex to a record ₹12.2 lakh crore and moved urban policy from Smart Cities to "City Economic Regions" plus the Urban Challenge Fund; PMAY-U 2.0 sanctions keep arriving in large CSMC batches (16+ lakh houses by June 2026) and JJM has been extended to 2028 — but JJM in particular is generating contractor *payment-delay* news more than new-work news.

- GeM's works approach paper proposed starting with small civil works (buildings, pavements, drains) on an optional basis, with an "RFP Builder" — [GeM approach paper](https://admin.gem.gov.in/news/admin/showFile/ApproachpaperforenablingapproachtoprocurementofworksonGeM23NOV2020v121607670067.pdf)

## Q6. Candidate SEO topics (6–10) with evidence, competition and product tie-in
