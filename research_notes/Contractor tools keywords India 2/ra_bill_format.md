## 1. Official formats: CPWA/CPWD forms, measurement book, certificates, state PWD, private formats

### Takeaway
Several secondary sources agree that **CPWA Form 26 is the Running Account Bill**. It has three parts: Part I account of work executed, Part II secured advances (detail in Form 26A), and Part III Memorandum of Payments. **Form 23 is the Measurement Book**, and **Form 24 is the "First and Final Bill"** for one-shot payments. There is no reliable evidence for the brief's guess that "Form 31 = final bill". Sources say the final bill on a running account uses **Form 26 printed on yellow paper**, while a minority cite **27-B** (the 27-A/27-B series belongs to lump-sum contracts). I could not open the official CPWA Code, so all form facts are "consistent secondary", not "verified official".

### Cited Findings
**Form numbers and contents (secondary, but consistent across several sources)**
- CPWA form list: CPWA 24 = First and Final Bill, CPWA 26 = Running Account Bill, CPWA 26A = Account of Secured Advances — [ncertbookspdf CPWA forms list](https://ncertbookspdf.com/cpwa-forms-pdf/); also [TaxForm Enterprises CPWA & CPWD forms PDF](https://taxformenterprises.com/images/adminimages/pdf/1552726285_CPWA%20&%20CPWD.PDF)
- An index of CPWD forms lists the Measurement Book as Form 23 (with 23A as a standard measurement book) and the Running Account Bill as Form 26 — [Scribd "Forms No"](https://www.scribd.com/document/347514818/Forms-No-docx); a quiz document also gives Form-26 as the authorised form for an RA bill — [Tripura PSC "account without books"](https://tpsc.tripura.gov.in/sites/default/files/accountwithout-books.pdf)
- Form 26 is "used for all running payments to contractors and suppliers… The final payment of running work shall also be made in Form CPWA 26 printed on yellow pages" — [CPWA Code brief, pdfcoffee](https://pdfcoffee.com/cpwa-code-brief-pdf-free.html); same summary at [nsrcivil CPWA Code](https://nsrcivil.in/cpwa-code/)
- **Conflict:** a CPWA study guide puts the final payment on yellow paper as **CPWA 27-B**. Another source says 27-A/27-B are lump-sum-contract forms, so the two may describe different contract types — [PromotionExams CPWA Code notes](https://promotionexams.com/?page_id=12933)
- A Tripura "CPWD-26 (Revised)" bill is headed "CPWD-26(Revised)", cross-references paras 10-2-10 and 10-2-12, and its notes say yellow forms are for final payment, not intermediate payments — [Scribd check list](https://www.scribd.com/document/842551289/Check-List)
- Form 26 has 3 parts: Part I account of work executed, Part II secured advances, Part III payments (Memorandum of Payments). It needs signatures from the contractor, the officer preparing the bill and the officer authorising payment — [Scribd "overview of Running Account bill form 26"](https://www.scribd.com/document/459382455/overview-of-Running-Account-bill-form-26); [QuickBOQ CPWD Form 26 guide](https://quickboq.in/guides/cpwd-form-26-memorandum-of-payment/) (also a competitor page)
- Part I is a summary of the measurement book, so every quantity must trace back to the MB. Form 26A is the item-level annexure behind the Part II secured-advance totals — [QuickBOQ](https://quickboq.in/guides/cpwd-form-26-memorandum-of-payment/)
- Form 26 is "used for both advance payments and payments for measured works", with sections for work executed, certificates/signatures and a memorandum of payments (advances, secured advances). The final version is on yellow paper — [FormsPal Form 26](https://formspal.com/pdf-forms/other/form-26/) (commercial PDF site; unverified). Fillable copies also exist at [pdfFiller India Form 26](https://form-26.pdffiller.com/) and [DocHub secured advance Form 26A](https://www.dochub.com/fillable-form/44084-secured-advance-form-26a-cpwd-pdf)

**Rules (official or primary-hosted documents, seen only as snippets)**
- Running-account payments "should ordinarily be made monthly… treated as payments on account, subject to adjustment in the final bill". On the final bill the payee certifies the payment is "in full and final settlement of all demands" — [CPWA Code brief, pdfcoffee](https://pdfcoffee.com/cpwa-code-brief-pdf-free.html) (secondary summary of the CPWA Code)
- CPWD Works Manual 2019 (draft, cpwd.gov.in) defines **secured advance** as "an advance made on the security of the materials brought to site of work, to a contractor whose contract is for the completed item of work". It says stage payments (e.g. E&M) are not secured advances, and refers to SOP 5/17 for conditions — [CPWD Works Manual 2019 draft](https://cpwd.gov.in/WriteReadData/man_cir/38259E.pdf); final version at [NDMC-hosted CPWD Works Manual 2019](https://www.ndmc.gov.in/departments/Departments/Finance/nodal_cell/CPWD%20Works%20Manual%202019.pdf)
- CPWD GCC Clause 7: payments on intermediate certificates are "regarded as payment by way of advances against final payment only" — [Conditions and Clauses of Contract 2008 (ICTS)](https://www.icts.res.in/sites/default/files/media/attachments/files/Conditions-and-Clauses-of-Contract.pdf); same principle in [CPWD GCC 2019](https://www.cpwd.gov.in/Publication/GCC_Construction_2019.pdf) and [CPWD GCC 2023 (IIT Kanpur copy)](https://iitk.ac.in/iwd/file/2025/04-AC-EE-2025/GCC2023.pdf)
- GCC 2019 Clause 7A: "No Running Account Bill shall be paid for the work till the applicable labour licenses, registration with EPFO, ESIC and BOCW Welfare Board" are in place — [CPWD GCC 2019](https://www.cpwd.gov.in/Publication/GCC_Construction_2019.pdf) (snippet)
- Income tax is deducted "from all payments made to the contractor" (including advances against work done) under Section 194-C — [GCC 2008 (ICTS)](https://www.icts.res.in/sites/default/files/media/attachments/files/Conditions-and-Clauses-of-Contract.pdf)
- No running-account or final bill can be passed except on recorded MB measurements (secondary summary) — [InfraLens measurement book](https://infralens.in/pmc/billing/measurement-book-mb)

**PSU, state and other formats**
- BHEL tender: Running Account Bill "Form WAM 6" (Works Accounts Manual). Flow: "Total value of work done up to date (A) … Deduct value of work shown on the last Running Account Bill (B)" — [BHEL tender book 2](https://tenders.bhel.com/sites/default/files/book-2-vol-id_2-of-2-1581500316.pdf)
- UP Financial Handbook Vol V has a bill form (Form No. 34) that came up for the same query. Contents not verified — [UP budget FHB Vol 5](https://budget.up.nic.in/fin_h_book/volume5/part1/293.html)
- Odisha PPMS publishes real government bill and measurement PDFs ("merge_bill_msrmt") — [Odisha PPMS sample](https://ppms.odisha.gov.in/upload/merge_bill_msrmt/merge_bill_msrmt_1763615823.pdf)
- Bangladesh "P.W. Accounts Form 27 – Running Account Bill C" (a different numbering under the same colonial PWA lineage) — [Bangladesh portal PDF](https://file-barisal.portal.gov.bd/uploads/af33d299-24ca-4b79-903e-ae32d5450b10//63f/746/542/63f746542c21d931821386.pdf)
- Private / PMC convention: the contractor's billing engineer prepares the bill, and the PMC/client site engineer verifies it against the MB. The bill summary runs: gross value to date per BOQ, then extra items/variations, then materials at site (secured advance), then price escalation — [InfraLens RA bill template (beta)](https://infralens.in/pmc/billing/running-account-ra-bill)
- A subcontractor-side variant adds "materials issued by the builder" as a section to recover — [SiteSetu RA bill format guide](https://sitesetu.in/blog/ra-bill-format-process)

### Inferences
- The safe page wording is "CPWA/CPWD Form 26 – Running Account Bill (Part I work executed, Part II secured advances with Form 26A, Part III memorandum of payments); MB = Form 23; one-shot bill = Form 24; final running-account bill = Form 26 on yellow paper". **Do not claim "Form 31 = final bill".** Flag the 27-B conflict, or omit it.
- Government formats are cumulative ("up-to-date value minus previous bill value"), which matches the column logic of the TrueSiteSync export (Previous Qty / This Bill Qty / Total Qty). This is the main factual hook linking the product to the official format.
- The client bill (Form 26 style, with statutory deductions) and the subcontractor bill (adds material-issued recovery, back-charges, retention to the main contractor) are distinct. Few ranking pages separate them.

### Gaps
- I could not open the official CPWA Code or the Works Manual's Form 26 page, so exact column headings, certificate wording and para numbers are unverified. Someone needs to check a cpwd.gov.in or eprocure copy manually before publishing exact wording.
- State PWD numbering (e.g. Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Kerala PWD, MES, Railways) was not researched in depth.

## 2. Standard columns, sections and deductions of an RA bill

### Takeaway
The near-universal columns are: Item no / description / unit / BOQ (agreement) qty / rate / up-to-date qty / previous qty / this-bill qty, with up-to-date, previous and this-bill amounts. That is "this bill = (up-to-date − previous) × rate". After the gross amount come additions (extra items, secured advance, escalation), then GST, then deductions: retention/security deposit, income-tax TDS, GST-TDS (govt), labour cess, advance recoveries, LD, material recovery. I found no source that prescribes an official *ordering* of deductions. That gap is an opportunity, but any order on the page must be presented as convention.

### Cited Findings
- Standard formula: "this bill = (up-to-date − previous) × rate" — [ConstructionSupply.ai RA bill format Excel](https://constructionsupply.ai/ra-bill-format-excel)
- A typical RA bill has six sections: header, BOQ-wise progress, extra items, materials issued by builder, deductions, net payable — [SiteSetu blog](https://sitesetu.in/blog/ra-bill-format-process)
- Header fields seen: Name of Company, R.A. Bill No, Name of Site, Name of Contractor, Date, Duration, Work, Building — [Scribd Running Bill format xls](https://www.scribd.com/document/182426035/Running-Bill-Formate-xls). Government-style header: name of work, agency, agreement no. and date, then an abstract with item no., description, unit, rate, qty and amount to date and for the period — [Scribd RA Bill Format (MNIT Jaipur sample)](https://www.scribd.com/document/251311107/RA-Bill-Format)
- MB abstract: previous, current and cumulative quantities with rate and amount per item, feeding the RA bill — [InfraLens MB](https://infralens.in/pmc/billing/measurement-book-mb)
- Typical attachments: abstract of BOQ and joint measurement sheets — [Scribd checklist](https://www.scribd.com/document/783888093/Checklist)
- Certificate text (template): work done as per plan, design, specification and terms of contract, and the claimed amount is correct — [Scribd running account bill](https://www.scribd.com/document/615986354/Running-account-bill) (user upload)
- **Retention / security deposit:** "typically 5-10% of bill value, released 50% at substantial completion + 50% at DLP expiry" (template convention) — [InfraLens deductions register](https://www.infralens.in/pmc/billing/deductions-register-ra-bills). One tender requires 5% of work value to be deducted from each RA bill as security — cited in [Running Bill guide, constructionestimatorindia](https://constructionestimatorindia.com/?p=11387)
- **Income-tax TDS:** 1% individual/HUF, 2% others; threshold above ₹30,000 per payment or ₹1,00,000 a year. From 1 April 2026, under the Income-tax Act 2025, the old s.194C maps to **s.393(1) Table Sl. No. 6(i)** with rates unchanged — [TDSMAN blog (Jun 2026)](https://blog.tdsman.com/2026/06/tds-on-payments-to-contractors-section-3931-section-194c/); [Aditya Birla Capital s.393](https://www.adityabirlacapital.com/abc-of-money/section-393-tds-on-contractor). These are secondary, so check CBDT before quoting. **Note:** the site's existing pages say "TDS 194C"; a 2026 page should say "s.393 (formerly 194C)".
- **GST TDS (s.51 CGST Act):** deducted by government departments, local authorities and notified bodies on taxable contract value above ₹2.5 lakh. Rate 2% (1% CGST + 1% SGST or 2% IGST); deposited by the 10th with GSTR-7 — [Masters India](https://www.mastersindia.co/blog/gst-tds-and-tcs-sections-51-52/); threshold also in [Haryana AAR order, gstcouncil.gov.in](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/AAR/har_aar_26_2019-20_26.06.2020_kscbpl.pdf); state-law wording ("one per cent" under OGST = state half) in [Odisha finance circular](https://finance.odisha.gov.in/sites/default/files/finance_circulars/21985.pdf)
- **Labour (BOCW) cess:** levied at 1% of cost of construction under the BOCW Cess Act, 1996 (the Act allows 1–2%). It is the contractor's (employer's) liability, deducted at source from bills as provisional collection — [West Bengal BOCW cess FAQ](https://lc.wb.gov.in/sites/default/files/upload/faq/bocww-cess-faq.pdf). The Supreme Court held that cess cannot be levied or collected where no Welfare Board was constituted (NHAI's 1% deductions from running bills) — [KS&K summary](https://ksandk.com/adr/bocw-cess-levy-requires-welfare-board/)
- **Mobilisation advance recovery and TDS** appear in sample deduction registers. That register cites FIDIC Sub-clause 14.6, which requires deductions to be itemised — [InfraLens deductions register](https://www.infralens.in/pmc/billing/deductions-register-ra-bills)
- **Secured advance** goes in Part II (with Form 26A detail) and is recovered as the materials are consumed in work — [QuickBOQ](https://quickboq.in/guides/cpwd-form-26-memorandum-of-payment/); definition in [CPWD Works Manual 2019 draft](https://cpwd.gov.in/WriteReadData/man_cir/38259E.pdf)

### Inferences
- A defensible "net payable waterfall" for the page, labelled convention, not statute: (A) up-to-date value → (B) less previous bill value → (C) this-bill value (+ extra items, + escalation, ± secured advance net of recovery) → (D) add GST (CGST+SGST or IGST) → (E) deductions: income-tax TDS on the value excluding GST, GST-TDS (govt clients only), labour cess, retention/SD, mobilisation/machinery advance recovery, material issued recovery, LD/penalties, other recoveries → (F) net payable. The TDS base excluding GST follows CBDT practice, but I did not verify a source for it here, so this needs checking.
- TrueSiteSync's export columns (Sr No, Item No, Description, UOM, BOQ/PO Qty, Previous, This Bill, Total, rate, amounts) map 1:1 to the Form 26 Part I logic. The page can show a "Form 26 ↔ Excel ↔ TrueSiteSync column mapping" table.

### Gaps
- I found no authoritative document fixing the deduction sequence or the TDS/retention base (gross vs pre-GST). Contracts vary.
- The labour cess deduction wording in the CPWD Works Manual (a section exists per the 2014 manual title) was not readable — [CPWD Works Manual 2014 (IITB)](http://www.iitb.ac.in/deanpl/worksmanual2014.pdf).
