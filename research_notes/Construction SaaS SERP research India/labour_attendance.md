## Q5. Legal context: muster roll and wage registers under BOCW and the Labour Codes; status in 2026

### Takeaway
All four Labour Codes took effect on 21 November 2025, which is verified by EY, KPMG and HSF Kramer alerts. The final Central Rules under all four codes were notified around 8 May 2026 according to law-firm alerts. Under the central rules, the attendance register and muster roll are combined (Form IX "Attendance Register cum Muster Roll"), and Form IV is the register of wages, overtime, advances, fines and deductions. Electronic maintenance is reported to be permitted. The rules for most construction sites are set by states, and state rule status varies.

### Cited Findings
- The Code on Wages 2019, Code on Social Security 2020, OSH Code 2020 and IR Code 2020 became effective on 21 November 2025. They replace 29 central labour laws — [EY alert PDF](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-in/alerts-hub/2025/11/new-labour-codes-implemented-across-the-country-effective-21-november-2025.pdf); [KPMG Flash](https://www.in.kpmg.com/taxflashnews/KPMG-Flash-News-GOI-notifies-implementation-of-Labour-Codes.pdf); [HSF Kramer](https://www.hsfkramer.com/notes/employment/2025-posts/india-labour-codes-implemented-a-landmark-reform); [BDO](https://www.bdo.in/en-gb/insights/alerts-updates/alert-implementation-of-labour-codes-key-provisions-notified-effective-21-november-2025)
- The Wage Code and Social Security Code were brought into force only in part, while the IR and OSH Codes came into force in full. Rules were still pending at that time — [Lexplosion](https://lexplosion.in/labour-codes-notified-with-effect-from-21st-november-2025-rules-yet-to-gain-effect/)
- Existing laws and rules continued during the transition — [Leather India / CLE circular](https://leatherindia.org/government-announces-implementation-of-four-labour-codes-with-effect-from-21st-november-2025/)
- Final Central Rules under all four codes were notified on 8–9 May 2026. Procedural compliances (registers, forms, returns) are operational at the central level — [BDO final rules](https://www.bdo.in/en-gb/insights/alerts-updates/alert-final-central-rules-notified-under-all-four-labour-codes); [Nishith Desai](https://www.nishithdesai.com/default.aspx?id=15591); [Mondaq FAQ](https://www.mondaq.com/india/employee-rights-labour-relations/1797080/faqs-on-the-recently-implemented-central-rules-under-indias-new-labour-codes). Contradicted by an older LKS page saying the central Wage Code rules were pending and predecessor registers continued — [LKS registers](https://employmentlaw.lkslaw.com/registers). The LKS page is likely outdated.
- OSH (Central) Rules, 2026 were notified on 8 May 2026 and apply where the Centre is the appropriate government (for example central PSUs, mines, major ports and their contractors) — summary via search of [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/employee-rights-labour-relations/1797080/faqs-on-the-recently-implemented-central-rules-under-indias-new-labour-codes) / [Nishith Desai](https://www.nishithdesai.com/default.aspx?id=15591)
- Central Rules forms: Form I Employee Register; Form IV Register of Wages, Overtime, Advances, Fines and Deductions for Damages and Loss; Form IX Attendance Register cum Muster Roll — [BDO final rules](https://www.bdo.in/en-gb/insights/alerts-updates/alert-final-central-rules-notified-under-all-four-labour-codes)
- The MoLE compliance handbook (as reported) allows electronic maintenance of registers and electronic filing of the annual return — [Kredily attendance register](https://kredily.com/attendance-register/) (secondary source; MoLE primary not retrieved)
- Legacy BOCW form example: HP BOCW Form XVIII "Register of Wages-cum-Muster Roll" under rule 241(1)(a), with daily attendance, rates, deductions and signatures — [HP BOCW Board PDF](https://bocw.hp.gov.in/pdf/Form18June2012_A1b.pdf)
- The BOCW Welfare Cess Act, 1996 was repealed by section 164(1) of the Code on Social Security, 2020. Cess provisions moved to Chapter VIII (ss.100–108) — [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/analysis-bocw-cess-under-code-on-social-security/)
- The BOCW (RE&CS) Act, 1996 is among the laws subsumed by the OSH Code. Some registration and beneficiary provisions are reportedly handled under the SS Code — [Labour Law Reporter](https://labourlawreporter.com/bocw.asp); [Bombay Chamber repeal list](https://bombaychamber.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/labour-codes-repeal-E.pdf); sources are inconsistent on details
- The principal employer has joint and several liability for minimum wage compliance down the contractor chain under the Code on Wages (secondary claim) — [KSK](https://ksandk.com/employment-law/industries/construction/)
- Karnataka construction worker unions protested the repeal of the BOCW welfare acts — [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/karnataka/karnataka-construction-workers-union-protest-repeal-of-welfare-acts)

### Inferences
- Landing page copy should say "Labour Codes in force since 21 Nov 2025; Central Rules notified May 2026; state rules vary". It should not claim a specific statutory form for every state. Offering exports that map to Form IX (muster roll) and Form IV (wages, OT, advances, deductions) is a strong, defensible compliance hook, with "legacy BOCW Form XVIII format" for states still on old rules.
- The Form IV columns (overtime, advances, fines, deductions) line up with contractor workflows (kharchi or advances, OT). That is a natural bridge from the legal section to product features.

### Gaps
- Primary sources not retrieved: PIB press release, Gazette notifications, MoLE handbook text, and OSH Central Rules chapter on building and construction work. The legal team should verify these against labour.gov.in and egazette.gov.in.
- Floor wage amount and the current central minimum wage rates for construction (VDA revision October 2026) were not retrieved. Point users to the CLC(C) / clc.gov.in minimum wage notifications and their state labour department.
- Whether electronic registers are explicitly allowed in the text of the OSH and Wage central rules (only secondary confirmation found).
