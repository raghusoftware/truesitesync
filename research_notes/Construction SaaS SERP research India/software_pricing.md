## 3. Pricing models and hidden costs in the Indian market

### Takeaway
There are four models: **per user/year** (Onsite, Aasaan, Zoho, ACC), **flat per-company plan** (Powerplay, True Site Sync), **per project/site** (SiteSetu third-party listings, SuperWise, Buildrun) and **revenue/volume-based** (Procore ACV). Recurring hidden costs are GST, minimum seat counts, per-site add-ons, integration fees, implementation, training and AMC/renewal hikes.

### Cited Findings
- Minimum seat billing: Onsite requires a minimum of 3 users (₹36,000/yr floor) — [Onsite](https://onsiteteams.com/onsite-pricing/)
- Paid add-ons: attendance ₹20,000/yr; Tally/Zoho integration ₹20,000/yr + ₹5,000/yr maintenance — [Onsite](https://onsiteteams.com/onsite-pricing/)
- Per-site add-on charges of ₹500–₹2,000 per additional site — [SiteSetu](https://sitesetu.in/blog/construction-software-cost-india-2026)
- Implementation for Procore: CoreStart $5,000 for 12 weeks — [Projul](https://projul.com/blog/procore-pricing-analysis-2026/). Renewal hikes of 10–14% — [Projul](https://projul.com/blog/procore-pricing-analysis-2026/)
- ERP setup fees: BuildNext ₹2–5L one-time + ₹50k/yr; Ramco ₹5–15L + ₹2L/yr; Odoo ₹1–3L setup + ₹20–50k/yr — [search result summarising CEI ERP list](https://constructionestimatorindia.com/top-10-construction-erp-software-in-india/)
- ERPNext construction implementation: ₹1–4 lakh. Mid-range construction ERP total: ₹5–20 lakh/yr including implementation. SAP RISE: ₹30–50 lakh/yr — [SoftwareSuggest](https://www.softwaresuggest.com/blog/erp-software-price-india/), [vlocalgroup](https://vlocalgroup.in/resources/how-much-does-erp-cost-in-india), [Elitemindz](https://elitemindz.co/blog/construction-erp-software-pricing-india) (attribution among these three is not precise; the search tool merged them)
- Onboarding fees of ₹50,000–2,00,000 for larger tiers appear in one table — [SiteSetu](https://sitesetu.in/blog/construction-software-cost-india-2026)
- Foreign per-user tools at $30–50/user mean ₹25,000–40,000/month for 10 users — [SiteSetu](https://sitesetu.in/blog/construction-software-cost-india-2026)
- Aasaan asks buyers to get quotes based on "modules, sites, users, migration, integrations, training and support" — [Aasaan](https://aasaan.co/best-construction-site-management-software-in-india/)
- SuperWise's mobile app "will only work with active subscription" — [Aptoide](https://superwise.en.aptoide.com/app)

### Inferences
- A "hidden cost checklist" (GST, seat minimums, per-site fees, integration, attendance hardware, implementation, data migration, training, AMC, renewal escalators, data export on exit) is not presented completely on any ranking page. That makes it a strong content block.

### Gaps
- I found no source on AMC percentages specific to Indian construction ERPs. The industry-norm figure of roughly 15–20% of licence cost is unverified, so do not publish it without a source.

## 5. GST on SaaS subscriptions in India

### Takeaway
GST on SaaS / IT software services in India is **18%** (CGST 9% + SGST 9%, or IGST 18%). This is widely reported, and the 18% rate for IT software was reinforced by Notification 13/2021-Central Tax (Rate). I did not reach a CBIC primary page that states it directly for SaaS.

### Cited Findings
- Notification 13/2021-Central Tax (Rate), dated 27 Oct 2021, amended Notification 11/2017-CT(Rate). It removed the 12% entry and the "Information Technology software" carve-out so that IT software is taxed at 18% — [ICMAI summary](https://icmai.in/upload/Taxation/Top_Stories/ITN/ITN_08112021_72.pdf.filepart); principal notification listing on [GST Council](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/node/4432)
- SaaS / cloud services are taxed at 18% for Indian customers under the OIDAR rules — [DigitalOcean India tax page](https://docs.digitalocean.com/platform/billing/taxes/ind/); [PayPro Global](https://payproglobal.com/saas-sales-tax/india/)
- Foreign suppliers selling to registered Indian businesses: reverse charge, so the buyer self-assesses IGST. Selling to unregistered buyers: the supplier registers via GST REG-10 and files GSTR-5A — [Xflow](https://www.xflowpay.com/blog/oidar-services)
- Vendors in this market quote prices "+ GST" or "+ 18% GST" (Onsite, SiteSetu, Powerplay) — [Onsite](https://onsiteteams.com/onsite-pricing/), [SiteSetu](https://sitesetu.in/pricing), [Techjockey Powerplay](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/powerplay)

### Inferences
- True Site Sync shows "GST as applicable". Stating "+18% GST" with the inclusive figures (e.g. Starter ₹5,000 → ₹5,900; Business ₹12,000 → ₹14,160) would match how competitors present prices and make the guide more transparent.
- The SAC code for domestic SaaS is commonly given as 998314 / 9983 (IT design and development / hosting services) or 998439 (OIDAR). Sources disagree. Confirm with a CA before publishing a SAC code.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve the CBIC consolidated rate schedule (cbic-gst.gov.in) to quote the exact heading and entry. Before publishing, cite the CBIC "GST rates for services" page or the GST Council notification PDF directly.
- The GST 2.0 rate rationalisation of Sept 2025 is not believed to have changed IT services from 18%, but I did not verify that.

## 6. Gaps a new page could exploit

### Takeaway
No ranking page combines a **neutral, dated, URL-cited competitor table**, a **total-cost-for-N-users calculator**, a **hidden-cost checklist** and an **official GST citation**. Current leaders are competitor blogs with unsourced or wrong rival prices.

### Cited Findings
- SiteSetu's rival prices (Onsite ₹1,500+, Powerplay ₹2,000+/mo) contradict the vendors' and directories' figures (₹12,000/user/yr; ₹71,999/yr) — [SiteSetu](https://sitesetu.in/blog/top-10-construction-management-software-india-2026) vs [Onsite](https://onsiteteams.com/onsite-pricing/), [Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/powerplay)
- Procore queries for India return only US-dollar pages; no ₹ explanation exists — [Capterra India](https://www.capterra.in/software/56250/procore)
- The small-contractor query is a near-monoculture of one affiliate site — [CEI](https://constructionestimatorindia.com/construction-management-software-for-small-contractors-in-india/)

### Inferences
- Opportunities:
  1. A "price per user per year" normalised column (e.g. TSS ~₹1,000; Onsite ₹12,000; Zoho ₹3,360).
  2. Worked examples for 1, 5, 12 and 25 users, including 18% GST.
  3. A "last verified" date and source link on every row, plus a "quote only" label for Procore, biCanvas, SiteSetu and ACC.
  4. FAQPage schema answering "Is there free construction software in India?", "Does GST apply?", "Per user vs per project pricing?", "What does Procore cost in India?" and "Powerplay price?".
  5. A dedicated Procore/ACC "in rupees" explainer.
  6. A disclosure that the page is published by True Site Sync. Neutrality and a disclosure aid trust and AI citation.
