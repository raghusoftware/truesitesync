## Q2. Top pages: structure, terms, tables, examples, schema, FAQs

### Takeaway

### Cited Findings
**Shivit — "BOQ vs Actual Cost in Construction" (2026-09-23)** — [Shivit](https://shivit.com/blog/index.php/2026/09/23/boq-vs-actual-cost-in-construction/)
- Defines BOQ (planned) cost as BOQ quantity × BOQ rate, and calls the gap to actual "construction cost variance".
- Formulas: Cost Variance = Actual Cost − Planned (BOQ) Cost; Variance % = (Actual − Planned) ÷ Planned × 100. Note the sign convention: positive means overrun, which is the opposite of the PMI EV convention.
- Worked figure in a graphic: ₹5.40 Cr planned vs ₹5.776 Cr actual, a variance of ₹37.6 lakh (about 7%).
- Partial-completion rule: at 40% completion, compare actual cost with the BOQ cost of that 40% of work, not the full BOQ.
- States that every variance traces back to quantity or rate, analysed separately.
- Distinguishes project budget (BOQ plus approved changes), committed cost (POs/work orders) and actual cost (consumption plus certified bills). Uses committed cost as an early warning.
- Word count, exact H2/H3 list, FAQ and schema: not retrievable (fetch blocked).

**Onsite — "BOQ vs Budget vs Actual: What Contractors Get Wrong"** — [Onsite](https://onsiteteams.com/boq-vs-budget-vs-actual-right-way-to-manage-construction-projects/)
- Argues contractors blame material and labour prices or client changes, while problems start earlier.
- BOQ, budget and actual are kept by different teams in different formats, so errors surface late.
- Definitions: BOQ = how many of each item; budget = quantities converted to estimated cost; actuals = confirmed site data (completed quantities, approved progress reports).
- Related Onsite page: progress reported as bare percentages has no BOQ or measurement backing, and line-item comparison beats project-total comparison — [Onsite BOQ](https://onsiteteams.com/products/boq-software-for-construction/)

**QuantBit BuildX — cost control software India (landing page)** — [QuantBit](https://quantbit.io/solutions/buildx/construction-cost-control-software-india)
- Tracks budget, commitments (POs, work orders) and actual cost per project and cost head. Pitches BOQ-to-billing continuity for Indian projects.

**biCanvas — Top 10 list** — [biCanvas](https://www.bicanvas.com/blogs/top-10-construction-management-software-india)

### Inferences (page blueprint opportunities)
1. **Formula block** (AI-citable, stated verbatim): Cost variance = BOQ value of work done − Actual cost (state the convention explicitly and show both). Quantity variance = (Actual qty − BOQ qty) × BOQ rate. Rate variance = (Actual rate − BOQ rate) × Actual qty. Check: QV + RV = Actual cost − BOQ cost. CPI = EV / AC. EAC = BAC / CPI. Cost-to-complete = EAC − AC.
2. **Worked ₹ line-item table**: e.g. M25 concrete, Fe500 steel, brickwork, plaster. Columns: BOQ qty, BOQ rate, executed qty (from MB), actual qty consumed (GRN − stock), actual rate (PO/invoice incl. GST treatment), QV, RV, total, and %. Separate wastage from scope change.
3. **Partial-completion rule** with a worked 40% example (Shivit mentions it, but the extract shows no worked numbers).
4. **Data flow diagram**: BOQ → indent → PO (committed) → GRN → issue/consumption → MB → RA bill → variance. India terms: MB, RA bill, GRN, SOR/CPWD DSR, GST, RERA.
5. **Free downloadable Excel/Google Sheets BOQ variance template plus an on-page calculator.** Nothing in the result set offers this.
6. **Offline-first site capture** angle (low-connectivity sites). No ranking page mentions it.
7. **Transparent comparison table** of Indian tools with stated methodology, plus "when Excel is enough".
8. **FAQ section with FAQPage schema** and a HowTo for "how to compare BOQ and actual cost".
9. **Credible macro stat** (MoSPI, dated correctly; see Q6) to anchor the "why".

### Inferences
- The monitored project set changes monthly (1,981 → 1,731), so the fall from ₹5.65 lakh crore to ₹2.88–3.41 lakh crore is **not** a like-for-like improvement. Completed or removed projects drop out. A landing page should cite one dated figure (recommended: "₹3.41 lakh crore cumulative overrun across 1,775 central projects, MoSPI, July 2026") and note that the figure covers only central projects of ₹150 crore or more.
- The "₹4.28 vs ₹3.4 lakh crore conflict" is a date mismatch: ₹4.28 lakh crore is a 2021 figure and ₹3.4 lakh crore is July 2026.
