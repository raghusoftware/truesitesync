How much does construction software cost in India?

Published construction software prices in India start at about ₹2,400 a year for a single user. Per-user plans from Indian vendors run roughly ₹3,000–15,000 per user per year, while global tools and enterprise ERPs are quoted in lakhs. GST at 18% is added on top, and setup, add-ons and renewal increases can matter more than the list price.

Construction software pricing models in India

  • Per user, per year or month. You pay for every login. Simple, but costs climb fast when supervisors and storekeepers need access, and field users are the ones who need the app most.
  • Per company plan with a user cap. One annual price covers up to N users. Predictable, and adding a site engineer costs nothing until you cross the cap.
  • Per project or site. You pay for each active project. Fine with few large projects, expensive as site count grows.
  • Volume-based or enterprise quote. Priced on your annual construction volume or negotiated with implementation. Common for global tools and ERPs.

Hidden costs to ask about before you sign

  1. Seat minimums (for example, a 3-user minimum).
  2. Paid add-ons for attendance, integrations or extra modules.
  3. Per-site or per-project fees on top of users.
  4. Implementation, data migration and training.
  5. Integration and maintenance fees for Tally or Zoho links.
  6. Renewal increases after a discounted first year.
  7. GST: is the quote inclusive or exclusive?
  8. Exit: can you export all your data in a usable format?

Is free construction software enough?

It depends on what "free" covers. A free trial is the best way to test a tool on your own site. A free attendance app is fine if attendance is all you need. Excel is free too, but it's where re-typing, version mix-ups and late month-end reports come from (see Excel alternatives). Avoid cracked or "lifetime free" downloads: your billing and project data are worth more than the licence fee.

Frequently asked questions

How much does construction management software cost in India?

Published prices start at about ₹2,400 a year for one user. Per-user plans from Indian vendors run roughly ₹3,000–15,000 per user per year, and global tools and ERPs are quoted in lakhs. Add 18% GST and any setup or add-on fees.

Is GST charged on construction software subscriptions?

Yes. Software subscriptions attract GST at 18% in India. Most published prices exclude GST, so check whether a quote includes it.

Is construction software priced per user or per project?

Both, along with per-company plans with a user cap and volume-based enterprise quotes. Convert every quote to annual cost for your real headcount, including site users, before comparing.

How much does Procore cost in India?

Procore doesn't publish prices. It quotes based on your annual construction volume, so you need a sales quote.

What does Onsite cost?

Onsite's pricing page lists Business at ₹12,000 per user per year with a 3-user minimum, Business+ at ₹15,000 per user per year, and Enterprise from ₹12 lakh a year, plus GST, as checked on 13 October 2026.

Is there free construction software in India?

There are free attendance apps and free trials. For billing, measurement, material and cost together, most contractors need a paid plan. Test a free trial on a live site first.

What hidden costs come with construction software?

Seat minimums, paid add-ons, per-site fees, implementation and training, integration maintenance, renewal increases and GST. Ask for each in writing before you sign.
