What is a muster roll?

A muster roll is the register of workers' daily attendance for a wage period, usually a month, used to calculate wages. Under the Labour Codes, employers and contractors must keep attendance and wage records and show them to an inspector on demand.

How wages are calculated

Days present = P days + 0.5 × H days
Wages = days present × daily wage
OT amount = OT hours × (daily wage ÷ 8) × 2
Net payable = wages + OT − advances

Overtime at twice the ordinary rate follows the Code on Wages; check your state notification and contract. Pay wages by the due dates: see Labour Codes for contractors.

Frequently asked questions

What is the format of a muster roll?

One row per worker with name, trade and daily wage, a column for each day of the month marked P, A or H, then days present, wages, overtime, advances and net payable.

How do I calculate days present with half days?

Days present = number of P days + 0.5 × number of H (half) days. For example, 26 P and 2 H = 27 days.

How is overtime calculated for construction labour?

Overtime is paid at twice the ordinary rate under the Code on Wages: OT amount = OT hours × (daily wage ÷ 8) × 2. Check your state notification and contract.

Is a muster roll mandatory for contractors?

Yes. Contractors and employers must keep attendance and wage records under the Labour Codes and produce them on inspection.
