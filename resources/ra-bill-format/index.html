What is the format of an RA bill?

An RA (running account) bill lists each BOQ item with its unit, contract quantity, up-to-date measured quantity, quantity paid in previous bills and quantity in this bill, with rates and amounts. Below the items come GST and the deductions (retention, TDS, recoveries, penalties) to reach the net payable. In CPWD work, the running account bill is CPWA Form 26.

The logic is always the same: this bill = up-to-date − previous. Quantities come from the measurement book, and the up-to-date quantity of an item should never exceed what the BOQ and approved deviations allow.

CPWA Form 26: the three parts

PartWhat it contains
Part IAccount of work executed, abstracted from the measurement book (the item table above)
Part IISecured advances on materials brought to site (detailed in Form 26A)
Part IIIMemorandum of payments: totals, previous payments, deductions and the net amount

Related forms: Form 23 is the measurement book and Form 24 the first and final bill for one-time payments. On a running account, the final bill is also prepared on Form 26. Running payments are advances against the final bill. Under CPWD's conditions, RA bills can be held until the contractor's labour licence and EPFO, ESIC and BOCW registrations are in place (GCC copy). See contractor licence and registration.

Frequently asked questions

What is the format of an RA bill?

A table of BOQ items with unit, BOQ quantity, previous quantity, this-bill quantity, up-to-date quantity, rate and amounts, followed by GST, deductions and the net payable.

Which CPWA form is used for a running account bill?

Form 26. Part I is the account of work done, Part II secured advances and Part III the memorandum of payments. The measurement book is Form 23.

How is the this-bill amount calculated?

Up-to-date value minus the value already paid in previous bills.

What deductions are made from an RA bill?

Commonly retention, income-tax TDS, GST TDS on government work, labour cess where applicable, advance recoveries, material recoveries and liquidated damages. Your contract decides which apply.

Is the final bill different from an RA bill?

On a running account contract, the final bill is also prepared on Form 26, with a certificate that it is in full and final settlement.

Can I make an RA bill in Excel?

Yes, using the columns above, but the previous-quantity carry-forward and deductions are where Excel errors creep in. Software that builds the bill from measurements avoids re-typing.

RA bill kaise banaye?

Har BOQ item ki naapi gayi up-to-date quantity se pichle bill ki quantity ghataiye, rate se guna kariye, GST jodiye aur retention, TDS, recovery jaise katauti ghatakar net payable nikaliye.
