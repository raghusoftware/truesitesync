In short. Procurement in construction runs: indent (the site asks) → purchase order (the office commits rate and quantity) → GRN (the site records what arrived) → purchase bill (the supplier invoices) → payment. Pay only when PO, GRN and bill agree. That's the three-way match.

The 5-step procurement flow

1. Indent / requisition

The site engineer requests material against a task or BOQ item, with the date it's needed.

2. Purchase order

The office fixes the vendor, rate, quantity, taxes and delivery terms. The rate is now locked.

3. GRN (goods received note)

The store or site records what actually arrived, the quantity accepted and rejected, with the challan and a photo.

4. Purchase bill

The supplier's invoice is entered against the GRN and the PO.

5. Payment

Pay against the matched bill. The vendor ledger shows what's due, what's paid and any advance.

The three-way match

Material delivered vs consumed

Stock on site = opening stock + GRNs received − material issued to work − transfers out. Compare the material issued with the theoretical consumption from the quantities done (cement per m³ of concrete, steel per the BBS). Anything beyond allowed wastage is a leak. Frequently asked questions

What is procurement in construction?

Buying the material, equipment and services a project needs: from the site's request (indent) through the purchase order, receipt (GRN) and supplier bill to payment.

What is a GRN in construction?

A goods received note: the record of what material actually arrived on site, how much was accepted or rejected, against which PO and challan.

What is a three-way match?

Checking that the purchase order, the GRN and the supplier's bill agree on item, quantity and rate before paying.

How do I track material delivered vs consumed?

Record every delivery as a GRN and every issue to work. Stock = opening + received − issued − transferred. Compare issues with theoretical consumption from the work done.

Can I get low-stock alerts for site material?

Set a minimum level for each item. True Site Sync shows each item's stock against that minimum and marks it Low or Out per site.
