Billing government and private work in Karnataka

1. Measure

Record work in the measurement book against the BOQ, which is priced from the Karnataka PWD Schedule of Rates (SR).

2. Bill

Make the RA bill with up-to-date and previous quantities and GST (state code 29).

3. Expect deductions

On government work, the department deducts the 1% BOCW cess, TDS and security deposit at source. Check each bill with the BOCW cess calculator and GST TDS calculator.

4. Chase payment

If you're an MSME, track the 45-day limit with the delayed payment interest calculator.

Plan around Karnataka's weather

Coastal and Malnad sites lose many days to the June–September monsoon. Bengaluru's October–November rain catches out slab and waterproofing schedules. Record the weather in every DPR so rain and heat days support an extension-of-time claim. See construction weather planning.

Frequently asked questions

Which construction software is best for Karnataka contractors?

Choose one that does measurement-to-RA-bill with GST, labour attendance and wages, material per site, and works offline. True Site Sync covers these from ₹2,400 a year.

What is the GST state code for Karnataka?

29. It is the first two digits of a Karnataka GSTIN.

Which schedule of rates is used for Karnataka PWD works?

The Karnataka PWD Schedule of Rates (SR). Check the department's portal for the current edition and any Government Orders that revise it.

Is BOCW cess applicable in Karnataka?

Yes. 1% of the cost of construction goes to the Karnataka Building and Other Construction Workers' Welfare Board, deducted at source on government works.
