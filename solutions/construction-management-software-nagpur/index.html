In Nagpur's infra work, machinery and material are the margin

Nagpur sits at the crossroads of central India's infrastructure push — highways, the Samruddhi corridor influence, industrial and MIHAN-linked builds, and the heavy procurement that comes with them. On this kind of work, the profit isn't made in the office; it's made or lost in machinery uptime and material control. An idle paver on hire, a roller that breaks down mid-laying, diesel that disappears, or aggregate that's over-ordered — these are the numbers that decide whether an infra job profits.

Yet plant and material are exactly what spreadsheets track worst. True Site Sync puts equipment utilisation, fuel reconciliation, preventive maintenance and live material stock in one system alongside RA billing — so the costs that actually make or break Nagpur's infra margins are finally visible and controllable.

Construction software in Nagpur — FAQs

Is True Site Sync good for Nagpur infrastructure and highway projects?

Yes. It focuses on the costs that decide infra margins — machinery utilisation, fuel, preventive maintenance and heavy-procurement control — alongside RA and GST billing, all in one offline-first platform suited to spread-out sites.

Can it track fuel and machinery across long project stretches?

Yes. It logs running hours and diesel per machine, computes litres-per-hour to flag theft or faults, and schedules preventive maintenance — whether the fleet is on one plot or spread over kilometres.

Does it help control heavy material procurement?

Yes. Material management reconciles indent, purchase order, GRN and supplier bill, so short-supply and rate drift on bulk cement, steel and aggregate are caught before payment.

Does True Site Sync work offline on site?

Yes. It is offline-first, so site teams in and around Nagpur can record measurements, material and progress with no signal, and everything syncs when they reconnect.

Is it built for Indian contractors?

Yes. It handles RA (Running Account) billing, BOQ item rates, retention and CGST/SGST/IGST natively — built around how Indian contractors actually work, not a generic global tool.
