The MB is your most important record — and your most fragile

The Measurement Book is where a project's money begins. Every bill, every payment, every reconciliation traces back to a quantity written in the MB. In government and PWD work it's a legal document. Yet on most sites it's still a paper register — handwritten, prone to damage, impossible to search, and readable only by the person who wrote it. Lose it to rain or a site fire and you've lost the basis of your billing.

A digital measurement book keeps everything the paper MB does — the same L×B×H entries, the same item references, the same running record — but makes it durable, searchable and connected. Every entry is tagged to its BOQ item, backed up to the cloud, and ready to roll into an abstract and a bill without being copied out again. The MB stops being a fragile record you defend and becomes a live asset you bill from.

The core idea: measure once in the digital MB, and that measurement is safe forever and billable instantly.

Measurement book software — FAQs

What is measurement book (MB) software?

It is a digital version of the construction Measurement Book — you record L×B×H measurements against BOQ items, quantities are computed automatically, entries are tagged and backed up, and they roll into abstracts and bills without re-typing.

Is it suitable for PWD and government work?

Yes. It keeps the standard MB format with item-wise entries and a durable, searchable record, so it suits item-rate government and PWD contracts where the MB is a key document for check-measurement and reconciliation.

Can I generate bills from the MB?

Yes. Measurements build into a priced abstract and convert straight to an RA bill or GST invoice, so every billed figure traces back to its MB entry with no re-entry.

What if I lose my phone or the register is damaged?

Your MB is cloud-synced, so unlike a paper register it survives a lost phone, rain or a site fire — the record is safe and restorable.

Does it work offline on site?

Yes. True Site Sync is offline-first, so you record measurements with no signal and they sync when you reconnect.
