JobTread is good — but not for Indian contracts

JobTread has earned its reputation in US light-commercial construction for clean estimating, budgeting and client management. The problem for an Indian contractor is not quality; it is localisation. Indian infrastructure and commercial work is billed through RA (running account) bills raised against a BOQ, verified with a measurement book, and reduced by retention money, TDS 194C and CGST/SGST/IGST. JobTread does not do any of that natively, so contractors end up bridging the gap with spreadsheets — exactly what software was meant to remove.

The difference: JobTread optimises the US estimate-to-invoice flow; True Site Sync optimises the Indian BOQ-to-RA-bill flow, offline, on site.
