Great for BIM. Not built for the contractor's billing reality

Autodesk Construction Cloud (ACC — Build, BIM Collaborate, Docs) is excellent at what it's designed for: BIM coordination, design collaboration, and document control on large, design-heavy projects. But most Indian contractors aren't living in a BIM model day to day. They're measuring RCC on site, raising RA-4, deducting retention, applying GST, tracking cement stock and chasing a payment. ACC isn't built for that money chain, and its cost and complexity assume a large enterprise with a BIM team.

True Site Sync is built for the execution and commercial side that ACC leaves to spreadsheets: measurement books, RA billing, BOQ and work orders, GST, materials, equipment and cash flow — offline on site and affordable. It's not a BIM tool and doesn't pretend to be; it's the tool the contractor uses to turn work into correct bills and visible profit.

The honest take: for BIM and design coordination, Autodesk is in its element. For a contractor's measurement, billing and site management, True Site Sync is the better-fit, lower-cost choice.

Autodesk Construction Cloud alternative — FAQs

Is True Site Sync an alternative to Autodesk Construction Cloud?

For the contractor's execution and commercial workflow — measurement, RA billing, BOQ, GST, materials, equipment and cash flow — yes. Autodesk Construction Cloud is focused on BIM and design collaboration; True Site Sync covers the billing and site-management side it leaves to spreadsheets, at far lower cost.

Does it do BIM?

No. True Site Sync is not a BIM tool. If you need BIM coordination, keep using Autodesk for that; use True Site Sync for measurement, billing, materials, equipment and cash flow — many firms run both.

How does the cost compare?

True Site Sync starts at ₹2,500/year with a free trial. Autodesk Construction Cloud uses enterprise pricing suited to large, design-heavy organisations, so for a contractor focused on billing and site ops the difference is large.

Does it handle Indian RA billing and GST?

Yes. RA (Running Account) billing with retention and Indian GST (CGST/SGST/IGST with works-contract SAC) are built in — areas a global BIM platform is not localised for.

Does it work offline on site?

Yes. It is offline-first, made for Indian sites with poor connectivity, unlike online-first design platforms.
