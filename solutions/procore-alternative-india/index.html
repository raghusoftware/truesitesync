Why Indian contractors look past Procore

Procore is a genuinely strong platform — the global benchmark for enterprise construction management. But that strength is aimed at large, well-resourced firms running big commercial projects in the US and similar markets. For a typical Indian contractor, three things get in the way: the cost (enterprise pricing, often quoted in dollars, that dwarfs an Indian firm's software budget), the fit (it doesn't natively speak RA billing, BOQ item rates, retention, or Indian GST), and the reality of Indian sites (patchy connectivity where an online-first tool struggles).

True Site Sync takes the opposite starting point. It's built from the Indian contractor's workflow outward — measurement to RA bill to GST invoice to cash flow — works offline on sites with no signal, and is priced so a solo contractor or a growing firm can actually afford it. It isn't trying to be Procore for the Fortune 500; it's trying to be exactly right for the contractor billing RA-4 this month.

The honest take: if you're a large enterprise standardising globally, Procore may fit. If you're an Indian contractor who needs RA billing, GST and offline site work at a sensible price, True Site Sync fits far better.

Procore alternative — FAQs

Is True Site Sync a good Procore alternative in India?

For most Indian contractors, yes. It provides the essentials they actually use — RA billing, BOQ, GST, materials, equipment and cash flow — works offline on Indian sites, and costs a fraction of Procore's enterprise pricing, which is aimed at large global firms.

How does the cost compare to Procore?

True Site Sync starts at ₹2,500/year for the Solo plan with a free 7-day trial. Procore uses enterprise pricing typically quoted in dollars and aimed at large firms, so for an Indian contractor the difference is substantial.

Does it handle RA billing and GST, which Procore doesn't natively?

Yes. RA (Running Account) billing with previous-vs-current quantities and retention, and Indian GST (CGST/SGST/IGST with works-contract SAC), are built in — the exact areas where a global tool like Procore is not localised for India.

Does it work on sites with poor connectivity?

Yes. True Site Sync is offline-first, so it works on remote and low-signal Indian sites where an online-first platform struggles, syncing when connectivity returns.

When would Procore still be the better choice?

If you are a very large enterprise needing deep global integrations, standardisation across hundreds of users, and US-style contracting workflows, Procore's depth may suit you. Most Indian contractors do not need that and are better served by an India-first tool.
