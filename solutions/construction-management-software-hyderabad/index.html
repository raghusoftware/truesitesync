Big projects magnify small record-keeping gaps

Hyderabad's construction is defined by scale — vast IT-corridor commercial builds around Gachibowli and the Financial District, and residential townships stretching toward Kokapet, Tellapur and beyond. At this scale, small record-keeping gaps become large money problems: a rate mismatch across a huge BOQ, a retention error repeated over dozens of RA bills, or material leakage on a site consuming truckloads a day. What a small project forgives, a large one punishes.

True Site Sync is built to keep large projects reconciled. Measurement flows into RA bills where previous quantities and retention are handled automatically no matter how long the BOQ, material is controlled at truckload scale, and cash flow stays live across the portfolio — so growth in project size doesn't outrun your control of it.

Yes. It is offline-first, so site teams in and around Hyderabad can record measurements, material and progress with no signal, and everything syncs when they reconnect.
