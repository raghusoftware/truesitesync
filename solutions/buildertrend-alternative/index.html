Why contractors switch from Buildertrend

Buildertrend is a capable platform, but it was built primarily for North American residential remodelling, and that shows for anyone doing commercial, infrastructure or heavy-civil work in India. Three frustrations come up again and again: the price (around USD 800/month billed annually is punishing for a small team), feature bloat where you pay for a big suite to use a few modules, and a scheduling calendar users describe as clunky. None of it speaks the language of an Indian contractor — there is no native RA bill, no BOQ-linked measurement book, no retention/TDS/GST handling, and no offline-first field app for sites with poor signal.

The gap: Buildertrend fits residential remodels in the US; True Site Sync fits Indian contractors who bill via RA bills against a BOQ and work on sites with no reliable internet.
