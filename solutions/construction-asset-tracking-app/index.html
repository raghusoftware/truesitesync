You can't protect what you can't see

A growing contractor accumulates assets faster than any register can keep up: machines bought and hired, tools issued and lost, materials received and consumed, formwork moved between sites. Spread across three or four active projects, the true answer to "what do we own and where is it?" lives only in the heads of a few key people — and walks out the door when they do.

An asset tracking app turns that scattered, tribal knowledge into a single live inventory you carry in your pocket. Every asset has a record — its location, who holds it, its condition and its cost — and every person on site can update it from where they stand. Loss, idle capital and duplicate purchases all shrink, because for the first time everything you own is visible in one place.

The core idea: one live inventory of everything you own, updated from the field — so nothing is lost, idle or bought twice by accident.

Asset tracking — FAQs

What is a construction asset tracking app?

It is a mobile app that keeps one live inventory of everything you own on site — plant, machinery, tools and materials — with each asset's location, custody, condition and cost, updatable from the field.

What kinds of assets can it track?

Heavy plant and machinery, small and power tools, and live material stock — all in one app, so every category of asset you own is in a single place.

Can the site team update assets from the field?

Yes. Anyone on site can update an asset's location, custody or condition in seconds from a phone, offline if there's no signal, and it syncs when they reconnect.

Does it show idle assets and total value?

Yes. The overview shows what you own, what is deployed, what is idle and what needs attention, along with asset value, so idle capital and losses are visible.

Does it need special hardware or tags?

No. It works on any smartphone with photo-based records — no scanners or RFID tags required to get started, though you can add your own asset codes.
