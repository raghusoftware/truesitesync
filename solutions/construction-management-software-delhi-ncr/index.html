Construction software in Delhi NCR — FAQs

Can True Site Sync manage projects across all of Delhi NCR?

Yes. It is built for multi-site firms — every site's RA billing, materials, equipment and cash flow across Gurugram, Noida, Ghaziabad and Delhi is visible per site and consolidated from one login, so management leads from live data instead of driving between sites.

Does it handle high-value, crore-scale RA billing?

Yes. RA bills across large commercial BOQs apply retention and GST consistently no matter the value, with receivables tracked live — critical when a single error repeats over crore-scale contracts.

Can it control heavy material and machinery at NCR scale?

Yes. Material is reconciled indent-to-GRN-to-bill at truckload volumes, and equipment utilisation, fuel and maintenance are tracked across scattered sites.

Does True Site Sync work offline on site?

Yes. It is offline-first, so site teams in and around Delhi NCR can record measurements, material and progress with no signal, and everything syncs when they reconnect.

Is it built for Indian contractors?

Yes. It handles RA (Running Account) billing, BOQ item rates, retention and CGST/SGST/IGST natively — built around how Indian contractors actually work, not a generic global tool.
