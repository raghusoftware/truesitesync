The DPR everyone needs and no one wants to write

The Daily Progress Report is the heartbeat of a construction project. The client wants it, the PMC demands it, the owner reads it, and disputes are settled by it. Yet on most sites it's the most dreaded task of the day — the engineer, already exhausted, sits down at 8pm to write out what happened: labour headcount, material received, work done, weather, delays. It's slow, it's often skipped, and when it matters most — a delay claim, a client query — the records are patchy.

The insight is that the DPR is just a summary of things the site already knows. If the engineer has logged the measurement, the material and the progress during the day, the report is already 90% written. A daily report app assembles those logged facts into a formatted DPR automatically — so the report is a by-product of doing the work, not a second job at the end of it.

The core idea: if it was logged during the day, it should be in the report by evening — with no one re-typing it.

Daily report app — FAQs

What is a construction daily report app?

It is an app that produces the Daily Progress Report (DPR) automatically from the work, labour, material and photos your site team logs during the day, so the report is a by-product of the day's work rather than a separate write-up in the evening.

Does the engineer still have to write the report?

Almost not at all. The app assembles work done, labour, material, weather and photos from the day's logs; the engineer only adds a note on any delay or issue, then shares it.

Can I share the DPR with the client and PMC?

Yes. The report exports as a clean PDF and can be shared with the client, PMC and owner in one tap, in a consistent format every day.

Does it help with delay claims?

Yes. Because weather, hold-ups and progress are recorded daily with dates and photos, you have documented evidence to support extension-of-time and delay claims.

Does it work offline on site?

Yes. True Site Sync is offline-first, so the day's activity is logged with no signal and the report syncs when you reconnect.
