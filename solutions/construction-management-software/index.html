What construction management software actually is

Construction management software is a single system that runs a project end to end — so billing, the site, materials, money and reporting stop living in separate spreadsheets, WhatsApp threads and paper registers. In practice that means: raising RA (running account) bills against a BOQ from a digital measurement book; capturing daily progress, material and labour on site; tracking inventory, equipment and petty cash; and seeing real profit per project — all connected, so a measurement taken on site flows to the bill and the P&L without re-keying. For Indian contractors the extra, non-negotiable requirements are handling retention, TDS 194C and CGST/SGST/IGST, and working offline where sites have no signal. True Site Sync is built around exactly these.

One system, on site and in the office: measure → bill → track cost → see profit, connected end to end and available offline at the workface.

Why Indian contractors need India-native software

Global platforms are powerful but built around US/global commercial workflows; generic accounting tools have no construction billing at all. Neither natively handles the mechanics an Indian contract runs on: RA bills against a progressive BOQ baseline, joint-measurement verification, retention released after the defect liability period, advance recovery, TDS under Section 194C, and CGST/SGST/IGST per work order. Bridging that with spreadsheets is where over-billing, lost retention and margin surprises hide. India-native software closes the gap — and because it must, it also works offline, since the sites that need it most have the worst connectivity.
