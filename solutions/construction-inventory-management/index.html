"Do we have stock?" should not be a guess

Ask a site engineer how much cement is on site right now and you'll usually get a shrug, a phone call to the storekeeper, or a number that's a week old. Material is the single largest cost on most projects, and it's managed on a register that lives in a locked cabin, a stack of unfiled challans, and a WhatsApp group where "send 200 bags" scrolls past unrecorded.

That gap costs money at both ends. Run short and the pour stops — labour stands idle, the RMC truck waits, the slab is delayed. Over-order and the excess sits exposed to rain, theft and wastage, tying up cash you needed elsewhere. And when material vanishes between the gate and the pour, there's no record to trace it against.

The core idea: every bag in and every bag out is one entry, so the closing balance on your phone always matches the stack on site.

How site inventory flows

Raise an indent

The site engineer requests material against the project — quantity, specification and needed-by date — from the phone.

Purchase & receive (GRN)

The purchase is raised, and when material arrives the storekeeper records a Goods Receipt Note against the challan, updating stock.

Issue to work

Material issued to a gang or activity is logged, so the closing balance drops in real time and consumption is tied to work done.

Reconcile & reorder

Closing balance vs reorder level triggers alerts, so you reorder before you run out and reconcile physical stock against the ledger.

Inventory, purchase and billing in one chain

Stock isn't a standalone book — it's fed by material management (indent → GRN → supplier), consumed against site progress, and paid for through purchase billing that flows into cash flow. Because True Site Sync links the GRN to the purchase bill and the issue to the work, the same entry that updates your stock also updates your costs and your payables.

It's part of the wider inventory & equipment management system, alongside equipment tracking, so stores and plant live in one place.

Construction inventory management — FAQs

What is construction inventory management software?

It is software that tracks material stock on your construction sites end to end — indents, purchase, goods receipt (GRN), issues and closing balance — so you always know what you have, avoid stockouts and over-ordering, and can trace where material went.

Does it track stock across multiple sites?

Yes. Each site has its own stock ledger, and you can see balances across all sites from one screen, so surplus on one site can be moved to another instead of buying more.

What is a GRN and does the software handle it?

A GRN (Goods Receipt Note) records material received against a supplier challan. True Site Sync captures the GRN at the gate, updates stock automatically, and links it to the purchase bill for reconciliation.

Can it warn me before I run out of a material?

Yes. You set a reorder level per material and the software alerts you when the closing balance drops below it, so you reorder before a stockout stops the pour.

Does it work offline on site?

Yes. True Site Sync is offline-first, so storekeepers can record GRNs and issues with no signal, and stock syncs when they reconnect.
