Distance and terrain make connected records essential

Jaipur is the hub for construction spread across Rajasthan — residential growth in the city, commercial and hospitality projects, and infrastructure reaching into distant, thinly-connected districts. Distance is the defining challenge: a site engineer might be hours from the head office on a stretch with barely any signal, and by the time paper records make it back, the information is stale and the chance to act is gone. Machinery and material move across long hauls where control easily slips.

True Site Sync is offline-first by design, which is exactly what Jaipur's geography needs. Construction software in Jaipur — FAQs

Does True Site Sync work on remote Rajasthan sites with poor signal?

Yes — this is its strength. It is offline-first, so engineers on distant, low-signal sites capture measurement, material and machinery data with no connection, and it syncs when they are back in range, giving the Jaipur head office near-live visibility.

Can it control machinery and fuel over long hauls?

Yes. Equipment utilisation, fuel-per-hour and maintenance are tracked per machine wherever it is, so cost and diesel loss on long-distance deployments are visible and controllable.

Does it suit hospitality and premium projects?

Yes. It produces clean, traceable RA and GST billing and documented progress suited to hotel, resort and premium residential work and their investors.

Does True Site Sync work offline on site?

Yes. It is offline-first, so site teams in and around Jaipur can record measurements, material and progress with no signal, and everything syncs when they reconnect.

Is it built for Indian contractors?

Yes. It handles RA (Running Account) billing, BOQ item rates, retention and CGST/SGST/IGST natively — built around how Indian contractors actually work, not a generic global tool.
