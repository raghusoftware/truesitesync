Billing government and private work in Telangana

1. Measure

Record work in the measurement book against the BOQ, which is priced from the Telangana Common Standard Schedule of Rates (SSR) from the R&B department.

2. Bill

Make the RA bill with up-to-date and previous quantities and GST (state code 36).

3. Expect deductions

On government work, the department deducts the 1% BOCW cess, TDS and security deposit at source. Check each bill with the BOCW cess calculator and GST TDS calculator.

4. Chase payment

If you're an MSME, track the 45-day limit with the delayed payment interest calculator.

Plan around Telangana's weather

Telangana's April–May heat makes concrete set fast and plaster crack, so pour early or late and start curing at once. The June–September monsoon stops excavation and external work. Record the weather in every DPR so rain and heat days support an extension-of-time claim. See construction weather planning.

Frequently asked questions

Which construction software is best for Telangana contractors?

Pick software that does measurement-to-RA-bill with GST, handles cess and TDS deductions on government bills, tracks labour and material per site, and works offline. True Site Sync covers these from ₹2,400 a year.

What is the BOCW cess in Telangana?

1% of the cost of construction. On government works it is deducted at source from contractors' bills and paid to the Telangana Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board.

What is the GST state code for Telangana?

36. It appears as the first two digits of a Telangana GSTIN.

Which schedule of rates is used in Telangana?

Government works are usually priced from the Telangana Common Standard Schedule of Rates (SSR) issued through the Roads & Buildings department. Check the current year's edition on the department portal.

What is TS-bPASS?

The Telangana State Building Permission Approval and Self-Certification System, the online system for building permissions launched in November 2020.
