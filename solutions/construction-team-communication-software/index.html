WhatsApp runs your project — and that's the problem

Every Indian construction firm runs on WhatsApp groups: one per site, one per client, one for the owners, and a dozen more. It feels like it works — until you need something. The approval for that extra work? Buried under 400 messages and a hundred good-mornings. The photo of the defect? Scrolled into oblivion. Who authorised that ₹2 lakh purchase? Nobody can find it. And when a key person leaves, the entire project history walks out with their phone.

The problem isn't chat — it's that WhatsApp is unstructured, personal, and unlinked to the work. Construction team communication software keeps the same speed and simplicity, but ties every message to a project, keeps decisions and approvals on record, attaches photos to the activity they document, and leaves a permanent audit trail the company owns — not a private chat that disappears with an employee.

The core idea: conversation about a project should live with the project, be searchable, and stay when people leave.

Team communication — FAQs

What is construction team communication software?

It is software that gives construction teams structured, project-linked communication — organised channels, decisions and approvals on record, photos tied to the work, and a searchable audit trail the company owns — as a real replacement for scattered WhatsApp groups.

How is it better than a WhatsApp group?

WhatsApp is unstructured, personal and unlinked to the work: approvals get buried, photos scroll away, and history leaves with an employee's phone. This keeps conversation organised by project, makes approvals and decisions findable, and keeps the record owned by the company.

Does it capture approvals?

Yes. You can raise approvals for extra work, purchases or design changes, and the sign-off is captured on record and stays searchable, instead of being a verbal yes that no one can prove later.

What happens when an employee leaves?

The communication stays. Because it is company-owned and tied to projects, a resignation does not erase the project history the way losing access to a personal WhatsApp does.

Does it work offline on site?

Yes. True Site Sync is offline-first, so messages and photos queue when there is no signal and sync when you reconnect.
