Construction GST is where generic invoicing tools quietly fail

GST on construction isn't simple invoicing. It's intra-state versus inter-state deciding CGST/SGST or IGST. It's the right SAC code for works contracts. It's Reverse Charge Mechanism on certain supplies. It's tax interacting with retention on a Running Account bill. Get any of it wrong and the invoice is queried, input credit is lost, or a mismatch shows up in reconciliation months later. Generic invoicing tools — built for a shop selling products — don't model any of this.

GST billing software built for contractors bakes these rules in. It applies CGST/SGST or IGST based on place of supply, carries the SAC you've set per work order, handles RCM where relevant, and layers tax correctly on RA bills with retention. The result is a tax invoice that's compliant by construction, so your filing is clean and your credits aren't lost to avoidable errors.

The core idea: the GST logic of a works contract should be built into the bill — not re-decided, and re-risked, on every invoice.

GST billing for contractors — FAQs

What is GST billing software for contractors?

It is billing software that applies construction GST rules correctly — CGST/SGST or IGST by place of supply, the right works-contract SAC, RCM where applicable, and tax layered correctly on RA bills with retention — so your tax invoices are compliant and reconcile to your returns.

Does it decide CGST/SGST vs IGST automatically?

Yes. Based on the place of supply set on the work order, it applies CGST/SGST for intra-state and IGST for inter-state billing, removing the most common place-of-supply error.

Does it handle SAC codes and RCM?

Yes. You set the works-contract SAC per work order so it appears correctly on every invoice, and Reverse Charge Mechanism is handled where it applies.

Does it handle GST together with retention on RA bills?

Yes. It applies tax and deducts retention correctly on Running Account bills, so each RA bill is compliant and there is nothing for the client's QS to query.

Does it work offline on site?

Yes. True Site Sync is offline-first — you measure and bill from site with no signal, and it syncs when you reconnect.
