Your machines are your second-biggest cost — and the least tracked

On most Indian construction sites, plant and machinery is the second-largest cost after material and labour — yet it's tracked the least. A hired excavator can sit idle for four days waiting for a soil report, but the hire meter keeps running. A mixer breaks down and the operator "informs someone", but no one logs it, so the same fault recurs three sites later. Diesel goes into a machine, but the logbook is a torn register in the operator's cabin.

The result is money leaking in ways nobody can see: paying off-hire rates for machines doing nothing, over-paying fuel bills that don't match running hours, and buying new equipment when the one you own is 40% under-utilised on another site. Without a live equipment register, none of this is visible until the numbers are already spent.

The core idea: every machine should have a live logbook — hours, fuel, location and condition — that the site engineer updates in seconds and the owner sees in real time.

How equipment tracking works

Add every machine once

Owned or hired, with its registration, hire rate, and the site it starts on. Photos and documents (RC, insurance, PUC) attach to the machine.

Log the daily entry on site

The operator or engineer records opening/closing meter reading, diesel filled and any breakdown — from a phone, offline, in seconds.

See utilisation live

Running hours, idle days and litres-per-hour are computed automatically, per machine and per site, so waste stands out immediately.

Move, service and off-hire on time

When a machine goes idle, you see it the same day — transfer it to a hungry site or return it before another day of hire is charged.

Construction equipment management — FAQs

What is construction equipment management software?

It is software that keeps a live register of every machine on your sites — running hours, fuel, breakdowns, location and cost — so you can maximise utilisation, control fuel and hire costs, and know the true cost of plant on each project.

Can it track both owned and hired machines?

Yes. You add each machine with its ownership type and hire rate, and the software tracks utilisation and cost for owned plant and hired plant separately, so you can reconcile hire bills and cost your own machines correctly.

Does it track fuel and diesel?

Yes. Diesel filled is logged against running hours, and litres-per-hour is computed automatically, so a machine consuming more fuel than normal is flagged before the bill lands.

Can it flag idle machines?

Yes. When a machine records no running hours it shows as idle, so you can transfer it to a busier site or off-hire it before another day of rent is charged.

Does it work offline on site?

Yes. True Site Sync is offline-first. Operators and engineers can log meter readings and fuel with no signal, and the data syncs when they reconnect.
