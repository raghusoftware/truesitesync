A fleet you can't measure is a fleet you can't manage

Plant is capital on wheels. A batching plant, a boom placer, a fleet of tippers — each represents lakhs of rupees that either earns through utilisation or bleeds through idle time and unplanned breakdowns. Yet on most sites, the only "plant management" is an operator's memory and a diesel register, and the only sign a machine is under-utilised is a hire bill that feels too high.

The two silent killers are idle time and reactive maintenance. A machine that isn't deployed still depreciates, still needs an operator, still costs interest on the capital. And a machine run to failure costs far more in downtime and emergency repair than one serviced on schedule. Both are invisible without data — and both are entirely preventable with it.

The core idea: measure utilisation and service every machine on schedule, and your fleet stops being a cost centre you can't see into.

Plant and machinery software — FAQs

What is plant and machinery software?

It is software that manages construction plant — batching plants, rollers, pavers, cranes and more — tracking deployment, running hours, maintenance, fuel and cost per hour across all your sites, so your fleet is utilised and serviced on schedule.

Does it handle preventive maintenance?

Yes. You set service intervals by running hours or date, and the software schedules the next service and reminds you before it is due, so machines are maintained on schedule rather than run to failure.

Can it compare owned vs hired plant?

Yes. Each machine records its ownership type and hire rate, and the software shows utilisation and cost per hour, so you can make buy-vs-hire decisions with real data.

Does it show plant utilisation?

Yes. Running hours are compared against available hours per machine and per project, so idle plant and under-utilised capital are immediately visible.

Does it work offline on site?

Yes. True Site Sync is offline-first, so hours and maintenance can be logged with no signal and sync when you reconnect.
