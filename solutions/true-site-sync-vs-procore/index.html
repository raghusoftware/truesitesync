Bottom line: which one should you choose?

Choose Procore if you are a large general contractor or developer running enterprise-scale commercial projects, with an IT team to administer the platform and a budget in the range of USD 800–1,000+ per month. Its depth, integrations and global ecosystem are genuinely best-in-class at that scale. Choose True Site Sync if you run a 10–50 person contracting or specialised infrastructure business in India, bill through RA bills against a BOQ, deal with retention money, TDS and GST, and need site engineers to log daily work on a phone — often with no internet. True Site Sync starts at ₹2,400/year with no implementation fee, where Procore's localised setup for these workflows is costly and heavy.

In one line: Procore is enterprise software adapted to any market; True Site Sync is built natively for the Indian construction site and the contractor's cash flow.

Where Procore wins

It would be dishonest to pretend Procore has no advantages. For very large organisations it is a deeper platform: an enormous integration marketplace, mature drawing and RFI/submittal management, sophisticated role hierarchies, and a global support and partner network. If you employ hundreds of people, run US-style commercial contracts, and have staff whose full-time job is to administer software, Procore's breadth is hard to match and worth its price.
