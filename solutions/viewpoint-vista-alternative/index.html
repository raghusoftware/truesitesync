Powerful, but built for a different world

Viewpoint Vista (Trimble) is a serious construction ERP with deep accounting, and for very large US contractors it earns its place. The complaint most teams have is that it feels like a financial system adapted to construction rather than a construction tool: a dense, complex interface, a steep learning curve, real implementation cost, and — for Indian firms — no native handling of RA (running account) billing, BOQ-linked measurement books, retention money, TDS 194C or CGST/SGST/IGST. Worst of all, it lives in the office; the person actually executing the work on site can't easily use it, so the field falls back to paper and WhatsApp.

The mismatch: Vista optimises head-office finance for enterprises; Indian contractors need field-first RA/BOQ billing that works offline on site.
