Contractor Licence in India 2026: PWD, CPWD registration and the new labour licence

Many contractor licence guides still quote rules that changed when the Labour Codes took effect in November 2025. This guide sets out what you need to work on government and private projects in 2026, and why missing registrations can hold up your RA bills.

What licences does a contractor need in India?

To work on government projects, a contractor usually needs registration with the state PWD (or CPWD enlistment for central work), GST and PAN registration, and EPF and ESIC registration for their workforce. Under the Labour Codes, a contractor deploying 50 or more contract workers also needs a single labour licence, which replaced the old Contract Labour Act licence.

State PWD contractor registration

Each state registers contractors in classes, such as Class I to Class V or A to E, with a maximum tender value for each class. Higher classes need more experience, turnover, engineers on staff and equipment. Requirements and fees vary by state, so use your state PWD's current rules and forms. You can usually move up a class by showing completed work and turnover.

CPWD enlistment

For central government works, contractors enlist with CPWD in categories and classes through its online enlistment system. Enlistment is checked against completed work, financial capacity, technical staff and equipment, and must be renewed.

The labour licence under the OSH Code

The Contract Labour (Regulation and Abolition) Act no longer applies. It was replaced by the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code from 21 November 2025. As reported by compliance firms, contractors deploying 50 or more contract workers now need a single licence, valid for five years and applied for online, rather than a licence per establishment (Lexplosion; factoHR).

Guides that still say "licence needed for 20+ workers" are describing the old Act. State rules decide the details for most sites, so confirm with your state labour department. More in Labour Codes for contractors.

Why missing registrations hold up your RA bill

Government contracts increasingly tie payment to compliance. For example, Puducherry PWD's General Conditions of Contract (2023) link RA bill payment to the labour licence and EPFO, ESIC and BOCW registration (Puducherry PWD GCC).

If a ₹12,00,000 RA bill is held for 60 days while you get registered, the financing cost at 12% a year is about ₹23,671 (₹12,00,000 × 12% × 60 ÷ 365), more than the effort of registering first.

Documents checklist

  • PAN, GST registration, and Udyam registration if you're an MSME
  • Firm documents: partnership deed, incorporation certificate or proprietorship proof
  • Bank solvency certificate and audited turnover statements
  • Work-done and completion certificates from clients
  • List of engineers and staff, with qualifications
  • List of owned or leased equipment
  • EPF and ESIC registration, and labour licence where applicable
  • Address proof, photographs and authorised signatory details

Each state and department publishes its own list. Use it as the final word.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a labour licence with fewer than 50 workers?

Under the OSH Code, the single contractor licence is reported to apply to contractors deploying 50 or more contract workers. Below that, check your state's rules and your contract's requirements.

Is the old Contract Labour Act licence still valid?

The Contract Labour Act was replaced by the OSH Code from 21 November 2025. Check your state's transition rules for existing licences.

How long is the new contractor licence valid?

Compliance firms report five years for the single licence under the OSH Code. Confirm with your state labour department.

How do I register as a PWD contractor?

Apply to your state PWD in the class that matches the tender values you want, with experience, turnover, staff and equipment documents. Requirements and fees differ by state.

What is CPWD enlistment?

It is CPWD's registration of contractors in categories and classes for central government works, applied for through its online enlistment system.

Can my RA bill be held for missing registrations?

Yes. Some contracts, such as Puducherry PWD's 2023 conditions, link RA bill payment to the labour licence and EPFO, ESIC and BOCW registration.

Contractor license kaise banaye?

Rajya PWD me class ke hisaab se registration karein, GST-PAN aur EPF-ESIC lein, aur 50 se zyada contract mazdoor hon to naye labour code ke tahat ek labour licence lein, jo 5 saal ke liye maanya hai.
