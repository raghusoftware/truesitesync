What does labour attendance software do on a construction site?

Labour attendance software lets supervisors mark hajiri (attendance) for workers and gangs on a phone at the site, even with no network. Each day records present, half day and overtime. Wages, kharchi (advances) and deductions are calculated from it, and labour cost lands on the right project automatically.

It replaces three registers that rarely agree: the muster roll, the wage sheet and the cost report.

Where paper muster rolls leak money

  • Ghost and proxy attendance. Names that weren't on site still get paid.
  • End-of-day reconstruction. Attendance written from memory leads to wage disputes, with nothing to settle them.
  • Kharchi that goes missing. Mid-week cash advances noted on scraps of paper are forgotten at settlement.
  • No link to cost. You know the wage total, but not which project or activity it paid for.

Labour Codes: which registers you need in 2026

All four Labour Codes took effect on 21 November 2025, and the final Central Rules were notified in May 2026 (BDO summary). The central forms include an Attendance Register cum Muster Roll (Form IX) and a Register of Wages, Overtime, Advances, Fines and Deductions (Form IV).

Most construction sites follow state rules, which differ in forms and timelines, so check your state's notified rules. True Site Sync's monthly muster and payroll records hold the data these registers ask for: days present, overtime, advances, deductions and wages paid. Export them as PDF or Excel for inspections. Frequently asked questions

Which is the best labour attendance app for a construction site?

The one your supervisors will use every day. It should work without network, take seconds per worker, handle half days, overtime and advances, and feed wages and project cost without Excel. Test it free on your worst-signal site before deciding.

Can construction attendance be marked without internet?

Yes, with an offline-first app. In True Site Sync, attendance saves on the phone instantly and syncs to the office when the phone gets a signal.

Is a digital muster roll valid under the Labour Codes?

The Labour Codes in force since 21 November 2025 prescribe an attendance register cum muster roll and a wages register, and are widely reported to allow electronic registers. Check your state's notified rules, and keep exports ready for inspection.

How do I deduct kharchi (advance) from weekly wages?

Record each advance against the worker when you give it. At payout, the advance is subtracted from the wages earned. True Site Sync's Bulk Pay does this automatically for each selected worker.

Can I link workers to a thekedar or gang leader?

Yes. Each worker can be linked to their contractor or gang leader (mukadam), or marked as direct, so gang settlements and labour contractor bills come from the same attendance.

How do I stop proxy attendance?

Mark attendance on site, on the day, rather than writing it up later. For staff, True Site Sync records punch in and out with GPS location and a photo.
