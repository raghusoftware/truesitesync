Small tools, big leak

Nobody tracks the ₹4,000 vibrator or the ₹2,500 grinder — until the third one this quarter goes missing and someone finally does the maths. Small tools and power equipment bleed money quietly: they're cheap enough to ignore individually, and expensive enough that a site losing a dozen a year is losing real money. Worse, when a tool "isn't on site", the fastest fix is to buy another — so you own five drills and can find two.

The register, if one exists, is a notebook the store-in-charge fills in when they remember. There's no record of who took the cutting machine to the terrace, so when it's gone there's no one to ask. Multiply that across sites and you have a category of cost nobody owns and nobody controls.

The core idea: a tool is issued to a person, not into a void — so there's always someone accountable for bringing it back.

Tool tracking — FAQs

What is tool tracking software for construction?

It is software that keeps a register of every small tool and power equipment on your sites — who it is issued to, where it is, and its condition — so tools are returned instead of lost and you stop rebuying what you already own.

Does it track who has each tool?

Yes. Every tool is issued to a named worker or engineer on a specific site, so there is always someone accountable for returning it, and you can see everything a person is holding.

Can it work across multiple sites?

Yes. You can run a central tool pool issued out to different sites and returned, and see utilisation and losses across all of them from one screen.

Does it flag overdue or lost tools?

Yes. A live list shows every tool that is out and which are overdue, so a tool held too long is chased before it is written off as lost.

Does it work offline on site?

Yes. True Site Sync is offline-first, so tools can be issued and returned with no signal and sync when you reconnect.
