The 5-minute daily routine

Morning

Mark attendance. Wages then calculate themselves.

When a truck arrives

Enter a GRN for what actually arrived, not what the bill says.

When cash is spent

Log the petty-cash entry with a photo of the bill.

Evening

Log machine hours and diesel issued, then send the DPR.

Frequently asked questions

How do I track construction expenses for multiple sites?

Record every expense against a site and a cost head on the day it happens, in one shared app rather than separate diaries. Review a site-wise cost report weekly and compare it with the BOQ budget.

What are the main expense heads in construction?

Material, labour wages, labour contractor, subcontractor, machinery hire, fuel, site overheads, staff, petty cash and statutory costs such as BOCW cess and insurance.

Which app is best for construction site expenses?

Choose one built for construction that ties expenses to sites, GRNs, attendance and the BOQ, and works offline. A general expense app records spending but can't show cost against work done.

How do I stop material money leaking?

Pay against a GRN, not the supplier's bill. Record what actually arrived, link it to the purchase order, and reconcile stock monthly.

Can site supervisors enter expenses without internet?

Yes, with an offline-first app. True Site Sync saves entries on the phone and syncs when the signal returns.
