High-rise, high volume, high scrutiny — Thane needs discipline

Thane has become one of the MMR's busiest vertical-growth corridors — towers along Ghodbunder Road, township clusters, and redevelopment feeding off Mumbai's overflow. High-rise work means long BOQs, many floors of repeating RCC, phased RA billing over years, and buyers and lenders who expect documented progress. It's high-volume work with Mumbai-adjacent scrutiny, and it rewards firms that keep tight, traceable records.

True Site Sync gives that discipline without the overhead. Measurement of each floor's work flows into RA bills with retention and GST, cash flow updates automatically, and every billed figure traces back to the site — so long, multi-year high-rise projects stay reconciled from foundation to finishing.

Is True Site Sync suited to Thane high-rise projects?

Yes. High-rise work involves long BOQs, repetitive floor-by-floor RCC and multi-year phased RA billing — exactly what the digital measurement book and automatic RA billing are built for, with previous quantities and retention handled floor after floor.

Can it manage several towers across the MMR?

Yes. You track billing, cash and progress across towers in Thane, Kalyan, Dombivli and the wider MMR from one login.

Does it work in tower cores and basements with no signal?

Yes. It is offline-first, so measurements and material logging work in core-shaft, basement and lift areas where signal drops, syncing later.

Does True Site Sync work offline on site?

Yes. It is offline-first, so site teams in and around Thane can record measurements, material and progress with no signal, and everything syncs when they reconnect.

Yes. It handles RA (Running Account) billing, BOQ item rates, retention and CGST/SGST/IGST natively — built around how Indian contractors actually work, not a generic global tool.
