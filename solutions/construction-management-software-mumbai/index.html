Mumbai builds under a magnifying glass

No Indian city builds under more scrutiny than Mumbai. Redevelopment projects tie you to societies, tenants and rehab-vs-sale accounting; RERA demands documented progress and fund discipline; and land is so expensive that a slipped floor or a short-paid RA bill can swing a project's viability. Add space-constrained sites where material can't be stockpiled and every delivery is just-in-time, and the margin for loose record-keeping is essentially zero.

Yet most Mumbai contractors still run this on Excel and WhatsApp — measurement in one file, RA billing in another, RERA documentation scrambled together at reporting time. True Site Sync replaces that with one connected system where measured work becomes an RA bill, the bill updates cash flow, and every figure is traceable — exactly the discipline this market punishes you for lacking.

Built for how Mumbai builds

Mumbai's redevelopment reality means multiple work orders, phased handovers and constant client and consultant queries — so traceability from a billed figure back to the site measurement isn't a nice-to-have, it's how you survive an audit. True Site Sync's measurement → RA bill chain gives you exactly that, and its offline-first design copes with basement and core-shaft work where signal drops. For space-constrained sites, live inventory means you order just-in-time without stocking out mid-pour.

Construction software in Mumbai — FAQs

Is True Site Sync suitable for Mumbai redevelopment projects?

Yes. It handles phased and multi-work-order billing with clean RA records and retention, and keeps every figure traceable to its site measurement — the documentation discipline redevelopment and RERA reporting demand.

Does it help with RERA-related record-keeping?

It keeps documented, traceable records of measurement, billing and progress that support the transparency RERA expects, though it is a project and billing tool rather than a RERA filing service.

Can it manage multiple sites across the Mumbai Metropolitan Region?

Yes. You run all your MMR sites from one login and see billing, cash and progress per site, so you are not travelling between sites just to know where each one stands.

Does True Site Sync work offline on site?

Yes. It is offline-first, so site teams in and around Mumbai can record measurements, material and progress with no signal, and everything syncs when they reconnect.

Is it built for Indian contractors?

Yes. It handles RA (Running Account) billing, BOQ item rates, retention and CGST/SGST/IGST natively — built around how Indian contractors actually work, not a generic global tool.
