Pune's growth is multi-site — and multi-site breaks spreadsheets

Pune's construction boom is spread wide: IT-park commercial builds, sprawling townships on the PMRDA fringe, and gated residential clusters, often several running at once for the same firm. That's the exact scenario where spreadsheet-and-WhatsApp management collapses — one file per site, one WhatsApp group per site, labour attendance in a register, and no single view of how the whole business is doing. The bigger you grow, the blinder you get.

True Site Sync is built for the multi-site contractor. Every project's measurement, labour, billing, material and cash lives in one platform, visible per site and across the portfolio. You scale from two sites to ten without adding a spreadsheet, because the system grows with you instead of fragmenting.

Built for how Pune builds

Township and IT-park work in Pune means large labour forces across phases, so tying attendance and deployment to the work being measured matters — it puts labour cost on the right activity and the right project. True Site Sync's offline-first field capture suits outer-ring sites toward Wagholi, Chakan and beyond where connectivity is patchy, and its multi-project view lets an owner in the city office see every peripheral site live. Live material stock across sites means surplus at one township phase can shift to another instead of new purchase.
