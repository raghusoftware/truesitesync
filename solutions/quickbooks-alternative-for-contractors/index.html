QuickBooks tells you the year; it can't tell you the site

QuickBooks is fine general accounting — invoices, ledgers, tax — but it was never built for construction. It leaves contractors blind to real-time job profitability unless you bolt on a separate, specialised job-costing tool, and it has no BOQ, digital measurement book, running-account (RA) billing, retention or advance recovery. So the commercial reality of a project — how much is executed, billed, retained, and whether this site is actually profitable — lives outside QuickBooks, usually in spreadsheets that never reconcile with the books. You find out a job lost money after it's over.

The gap QuickBooks leaves: no BOQ, no measurement book, no RA billing, no retention — and no live per-project margin. That's exactly the construction layer True Site Sync provides.
