A contractor's hardest job isn't building — it's getting paid right

The building work, you know how to do. The part that keeps contractors up at night is the money: measuring exactly what was done, billing it before the cut-off, carrying previous quantities forward, deducting retention correctly, chasing a payment that's forty days late, and knowing — actually knowing — whether this project is making money or quietly losing it.

Generic business software doesn't understand any of this. It doesn't know what an RA bill is, or retention, or a running account, or that your measurement book is the source of every rupee you'll ever bill. Contractor management software does. It's built around the chain that turns your work into your income — measurement to abstract to RA bill to payment — and around the costs (material, labour, plant) that decide whether that income is profit.

The core idea: the work you measure on site should turn into a correct bill, a tracked payment and a clear profit figure — automatically.

Contractor management software — FAQs

What is contractor management software?

It is software built specifically for construction contractors — handling measurement, RA (Running Account) billing, work orders, retention, GST, materials and cash flow — so a contractor can measure work, bill it correctly, track payments and see project profit, all in one place.

Does it handle RA billing and retention?

Yes. It generates Running Account bills with previous-vs-current quantity tracking, deducts retention at your configured percentage, and applies CGST/SGST or IGST — the core of how contractors bill.

Is it suitable for sub-contractors and labour contractors?

Yes. Sub-contractors can raise running bills against agreed rate lists, and labour contractors can track attendance and convert measured work into bills, keeping cost and billing together.

Can it show me if a project is profitable?

Yes. It shows billing minus cost per project, so you see estimated profit on each job at any time, rather than finding out at year-end.

Does it work offline on site?

Yes. It is offline-first, so you can measure and record on site with no signal, and it syncs when you reconnect.
