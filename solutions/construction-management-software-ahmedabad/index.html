Construction software in Ahmedabad — FAQs

Does True Site Sync show project-wise profit for Ahmedabad builders?

Yes. It shows RA and GST billing against reconciled material and machinery cost per project, live — so cost-conscious Ahmedabad firms can verify whether a sharply negotiated deal is actually delivering its margin.

Can it control heavy material and machinery cost?

Yes. Material is reconciled from indent to GRN to supplier bill, and equipment utilisation, fuel and maintenance give a true machinery cost per project.

Does it suit fast-moving commercial and residential work?

Yes. It turns site measurements into RA and GST bills quickly, works offline on highway and peripheral sites, and keeps a hands-on owner's whole operation visible from one platform.

Does True Site Sync work offline on site?

Yes. It is offline-first, so site teams in and around Ahmedabad can record measurements, material and progress with no signal, and everything syncs when they reconnect.

Is it built for Indian contractors?

Yes. It handles RA (Running Account) billing, BOQ item rates, retention and CGST/SGST/IGST natively — built around how Indian contractors actually work, not a generic global tool.
