Tracking is a start — but billing is where contractors get paid

Apps like Onsite have done a real service: they got contractors off paper for attendance, expenses and basic site tracking. But most contractors quickly hit the same ceiling — the app records what was spent and who was present, but it can't measure the work, price it against a BOQ, raise a Running Account bill with retention and GST, or tell them whether the project is profitable. So they still run their actual billing in Excel, and the two never meet.

True Site Sync is the comprehensive step up. It covers the tracking basics, but its core is the contractor's money chain: measurement book → abstract → RA bill → GST invoice → cash flow, with materials and equipment alongside. Instead of tracking activity and billing separately, you do both in one platform where the work you record turns directly into the bill you send.

The honest take: if all you'll ever need is attendance and expense tracking, a lightweight tracker is fine. If you bill on BOQ and RA and want profit visibility, you'll outgrow it fast.

Onsite alternative — FAQs

Is True Site Sync an alternative to Onsite (Teams)?

Yes, and a more comprehensive one. It covers the tracking basics like attendance and expenses, but adds the contractor's core money chain — measurement, RA billing, GST, materials, equipment and cash flow — that lightweight trackers don't handle.

Does it do attendance and expense tracking too?

Yes. You can track labour attendance and site expenses, so you are not losing what a tracker gives you — you are adding measurement, billing and profit on top.

What can it do that Onsite can't?

Record and value measured work against a BOQ, raise Running Account bills with retention, produce GST-compliant tax invoices, manage material and equipment, and show project-wise profit — the areas where trackers stop and contractors fall back to Excel.

Is it harder to use than a simple tracker?

No. It is built for site teams, works offline, and you can start with a free trial and one project. You adopt the extra capabilities as you need them.

Does it work offline on site?

Yes. True Site Sync is offline-first, so field capture works with no signal and syncs when you reconnect.
