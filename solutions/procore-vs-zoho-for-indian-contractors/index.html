The honest three-way answer

If you are comparing Procore and Zoho for an Indian construction business, the real problem is that they solve different jobs and neither solves yours completely. Procore is a deep, enterprise construction platform — excellent for large general contractors with IT teams, but priced in hundreds of dollars per month and built around US/global commercial workflows, so Indian RA billing, BOQ and GST need customisation. Zoho is the opposite: affordable and flexible, but a generic accounting and business suite with no native construction billing at all — no BOQ, no measurement book, no running-account logic, no retention or advance recovery. Between "too heavy and foreign" and "too generic," Indian contractors end up back in spreadsheets. A purpose-built tool closes that gap.

In one line: Procore = enterprise power, wrong locale; Zoho = cheap and generic, no construction billing; True Site Sync = India-native RA/BOQ billing, offline, affordable.
