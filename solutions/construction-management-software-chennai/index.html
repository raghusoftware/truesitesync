Construction software in Chennai — FAQs

How does True Site Sync help Chennai contractors with monsoon delays?

The daily progress report captures weather and delays each day with dated photos, giving you documented evidence to support extension-of-time and delay claims when monsoon stoppages occur — far stronger than a verbal record.

Does it help protect material in the coastal climate?

Yes. Live inventory lets you run just-in-time so less material sits exposed to humidity, and reorder alerts prevent both stockouts and over-stocking that would spoil.

Will professional OMR and industrial clients accept its billing?

Yes. It produces clean, GST-compliant, traceable RA bills and tax invoices where each figure links to a site measurement, meeting the documentation professional clients expect.

Does True Site Sync work offline on site?

Yes. It is offline-first, so site teams in and around Chennai can record measurements, material and progress with no signal, and everything syncs when they reconnect.

Is it built for Indian contractors?

Yes. It handles RA (Running Account) billing, BOQ item rates, retention and CGST/SGST/IGST natively — built around how Indian contractors actually work, not a generic global tool.
