Most site software fails the one test that matters: no signal

Site engineers have been given "site apps" before. They install them, take them to the basement of a tower or a highway stretch 30 km from the nearest tower, and the app spins forever waiting for a connection that isn't there. So the engineer goes back to a notebook and types it up that night — and the expensive software gathers dust. Any tool for site engineers that assumes connectivity has already lost.

True Site Sync is offline-first by design. Every measurement, every progress update, every GRN and every photo is captured and stored on the phone instantly, whether or not there's signal. When connectivity returns — end of the day, back at the site office, a moment of 4G — it all syncs to the cloud and the office automatically. The engineer never waits, never loses an entry, and never types the same thing twice.

The core idea: the app must work the instant you tap, signal or not — anything less and site engineers won't use it.

Construction app for site engineers — FAQs

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. True Site Sync is offline-first — measurements, GRNs, progress notes and photos are saved on the phone instantly with no signal, and sync to the office and cloud automatically when connectivity returns.

What can a site engineer record with it?

Measurements against BOQ items, site progress with photos, material received and issued (GRN and issues), labour, and the daily site report — all from a phone in the field.

Does it need a powerful phone?

No. It runs on any regular Android phone, and is designed to be light on data and battery, so it works on the phones site engineers already carry.

Does the engineer have to type up a report separately?

No. The daily progress report builds itself from what was logged during the day, so there is no second write-up in the evening.

Where does the captured data go?

It flows into the office and the rest of the platform — measurements into RA billing, GRNs into inventory, progress into daily reports — so the engineer's field entries drive billing and reporting with no re-entry.
