Billing government and private work in Maharashtra

1. Measure

Record work in the measurement book against the BOQ, which is priced from the Maharashtra PWD District Schedule of Rates (DSR).

2. Bill

Make the RA bill with up-to-date and previous quantities and GST (state code 27).

3. Expect deductions

On government work, the department deducts the 1% BOCW cess, TDS and security deposit at source. Check each bill with the BOCW cess calculator and GST TDS calculator.

4. Chase payment

If you're an MSME, track the 45-day limit with the delayed payment interest calculator.

Plan around Maharashtra's weather

Mumbai and Konkan sites lose many days to the June–September monsoon, so finish excavation and roofing before June. Nagpur and Vidarbha summers are among India's hottest, so plan night pours and longer curing. Record the weather in every DPR so rain and heat days support an extension-of-time claim. What is the GST state code for Maharashtra?

27. It is the first two digits of a Maharashtra GSTIN.

What is DSR in Maharashtra?

The District Schedule of Rates issued by the Maharashtra PWD each year, used to price government civil works.

What is MahaBOCW?

The Maharashtra Building and Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, which registers construction workers and runs welfare schemes funded by the 1% BOCW cess.
