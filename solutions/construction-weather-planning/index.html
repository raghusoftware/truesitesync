Record weather for extension-of-time claims

Rain and heat stoppages are common grounds for extending the completion date. Without daily records, a claim is your word against the client's. Log the weather and lost hours in the DPR every day. On CPWD-style contracts, also enter them in the hindrance register. Check your exposure with the LD calculator.

In True Site Sync, each daily progress report has a weather field that prints on the DPR PDF. Progress entries can be marked Clear, Cloudy, Light Rain, Heavy Rain, Hot or Windy, and schedule changes can carry "Rain / Weather" as the reason. Your delay record builds itself.

Seasonal planning by region

  • Southwest monsoon (June–September): most of India, and heaviest on the west coast. Finish excavation and roofing before it arrives. See Maharashtra and Karnataka.
  • Pre-monsoon heat (April–June): central and southern plateau states. Pour early and cure longer. See Telangana.
  • Northeast monsoon (October–December): Tamil Nadu and the south-east coast.

Frequently asked questions

Can concrete be poured in the rain?

Not unprotected. Rain washes out cement and raises the water-cement ratio. Postpone, or cover the whole pour area with sheets, and stop at a planned construction joint if heavy rain starts.

What is hot weather concreting?

Precautions when high temperatures make concrete lose workability and crack: pour in the early morning or evening, cool the mixing water, shade the aggregates and start curing immediately. IS 7861 Part 1 covers the recommended practice.

At what wind speed should crane lifts stop?

Stop when wind exceeds the crane's rated limit in the manufacturer's manual. It varies by crane and load, and large panels catch wind earlier.

Why record weather in the DPR?

Daily weather and lost-hour records are the evidence for extension-of-time claims and against liquidated damages. Without them, rain days are hard to prove.

Where can I get a reliable weather forecast in India?

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) publishes forecasts and warnings on mausam.imd.gov.in and in its Mausam app.

Barish me concrete dal sakte hain?

Bina dhake nahi. Barish se cement beh jata hai aur paani zyada ho jata hai. Pour aage badhaiye ya poori jagah tarpaulin se dhakiye, aur tez barish me construction joint par rokiye.
