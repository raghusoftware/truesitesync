Labour productivity calculator

Example: 10 workers × 8 hours = 10 man-days. 120 m² of plaster ÷ 10 = 12 m² per man-day, against a plan of 15. Expected 150 m², done 120 m²: a 20% gap. Labour cost = ₹7,000 ÷ 120 = ₹58.33 per m².

The formulas

Man-days = workers × hours ÷ 8
Output per man-day = quantity done ÷ man-days
Labour cost per unit = (workers × daily wage × hours ÷ 8) ÷ quantity done
Expected quantity = planned output per man-day × man-days
Gap % = (quantity done − expected) ÷ expected × 100

Where do planned output figures come from?

Your own past jobs are the best source: divide the quantity done by the man-days on two or three similar jobs. The Bureau of Indian Standards' IS 7272 gives reference labour output constants for building work, and many firms keep their own rate-analysis figures. Set one figure per activity and per gang, and review it monthly.

Frequently asked questions

How is labour productivity calculated in construction?

Divide the quantity of work done by the man-days used. Man-days = number of workers × hours worked ÷ 8. For example, 120 m² of plaster by 10 workers in 8 hours is 12 m² per man-day.

What is a man-day?

One worker working one standard 8-hour day. Two workers for 4 hours is also one man-day.

How do I calculate labour cost per unit?

Divide the day's labour cost by the quantity done. 10 workers at ₹700 = ₹7,000; ₹7,000 ÷ 120 m² = ₹58.33 per m².

How can I verify labour attendance before paying wages?

Compare the attendance with the work done that day. If output per man-day falls more than about 20% below your planned rate, check the headcount on site, the photos and the thekedar's muster before payroll.

Where can I find standard labour output figures?

Use your own past jobs first. IS 7272 from the Bureau of Indian Standards gives reference labour output constants for building work, and your rate analysis may have figures too.

Labour productivity kaise nikale?

Aaj ka kaam (quantity) ko man-days se bhaag dijiye. Man-days = mazdoor × ghante ÷ 8. Jaise 10 mazdoor, 8 ghante, 120 m² plaster = 12 m² prati man-day.
