Excel got you here — and it's now what's holding you back

Every contractor starts on Excel, and for good reason: it's flexible, familiar and free. But construction outgrows it. Your measurement sheet, BOQ, RA billing, material register and cash statement become separate files, maintained by different people, saved as "final_v3_latest.xlsx", emailed around, and copy-pasted between. The numbers drift. A rate changes in one file and not another. A formula breaks and nobody notices until a bill is wrong. And nothing is offline on site or safe if a laptop dies.

True Site Sync is the purpose-built alternative. It keeps the things you value about Excel — you still see your quantities, rates and totals in familiar tables — but the files become one connected system. Measure work once and it flows to the abstract, the RA bill, the GST invoice, the cost and the cash forecast. There's no copy-paste, no version confusion, and no reconciliation, because there's only one set of numbers.

The core idea: keep the clarity of a spreadsheet, lose the disconnection — one source of numbers instead of ten drifting files.

Excel alternative — FAQs

Why replace Excel for construction management?

Because construction outgrows it. Measurement, BOQ, billing, materials and cash end up in separate files that drift out of sync, break formulas, cause version confusion and can't work offline on site. A purpose-built tool keeps them as one connected, reliable system.

Will I lose the flexibility of spreadsheets?

No. You still work in familiar quantity-and-rate tables, but instead of ten disconnected files, they feed each other — measuring work updates the bill, cost and cash automatically.

Can I import my existing Excel BOQ?

Yes. You can bring your BOQ items in from your existing spreadsheet in minutes, then start measuring and billing against them.

How does it handle RA billing and GST that I do manually in Excel?

RA billing with previous-vs-current quantities and retention, and Indian GST (CGST/SGST/IGST with SAC), are built in and calculated automatically, removing the manual formulas and the errors that come with them.

Does it work offline on site?

Yes. Unlike a laptop spreadsheet, True Site Sync is offline-first and cloud-backed, so you work on site with no signal and your data is safe if a device fails.

How much does it cost?
