The gap between the site and the office is where money is lost

The site knows the truth: what was poured today, how much steel came in, which machine broke down, how many masons turned up. The office needs that truth to bill, to buy, and to decide. But between them sits a gap filled with delay — a diary that reaches the office at month-end, a WhatsApp update that scrolls away, a phone call half-remembered. By the time the office acts, the site has moved on.

Site management software closes that gap. Instead of the site writing things down to be typed up later, the site records directly into the system — measurement, progress, material, labour, equipment — from a phone, even with no signal. The office isn't waiting for a report; it's watching the site live. Decisions that used to wait for month-end happen the same day.

The core idea: the site records once, on the spot, and the office sees it instantly — no re-typing, no lag, no lost updates.

Site management software — FAQs

What is site management software?

It is software that lets a construction site be run from the field — capturing measurement, progress, materials, labour, equipment and daily reports on a phone, offline if needed — so the office and owner see the real site picture live instead of at month-end.

Does the site team need internet to use it?

No. It is offline-first, so engineers record work, material and labour with no signal, and everything syncs to the office and cloud the moment they reconnect.

Does it replace the daily site report?

It automates it. The daily progress report builds itself from what was logged during the day, so engineers do not write the report twice.

Can the office see multiple sites at once?

Yes. Progress, material and labour across all your sites are visible from one screen in the office, without phone calls or waiting for updates.

Does site data flow into billing and cash flow?

Yes. Measurements captured on site flow into RA billing and GST invoices, and material and payments flow into cash flow, so there is no re-entry between the site and the accounts.
