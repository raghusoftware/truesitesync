Why we built True Site Sync

Most contractors lose profit they never see — through missed measurements, delayed RA bills, untracked materials, and labour that quietly erodes margins. True Site Sync closes those leaks. It replaces messy measurement books, Excel sheets, paper registers and WhatsApp chaos with one connected system covering site measurements, running-account (RA) billing, inventory, labour, vendors and project profitability. Measure once on site (with automatic quantity calculations and BBS), generate GST-compliant invoices and RA bills instantly, track material purchases and consumption across multiple sites, manage daily-wage and contract labour, and get clear cost abstracts and P&L reports — so you always know which projects are actually making money.

Our promise: measure accurately, bill completely, track everything, and grow with confidence — without an expensive, slow enterprise ERP.
