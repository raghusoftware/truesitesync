# BOQ vs Actual Cost Software: See Overruns While You Can Still Fix Them

Your BOQ says what the job should cost. Your site decides what it actually costs. **BOQ vs actual cost software** keeps those two numbers side by side, every day, so you notice an overrun while there's still budget left to save.

> **What is BOQ vs actual cost software?**
> BOQ vs actual cost software compares a project's Bill of Quantities (planned quantity, rate and budget) with what is actually executed, billed and spent on site. It pulls actual material, labour and equipment cost from daily site entries. The variance shows up while the project is still running, not at final reconciliation.

## What does BOQ vs actual cost software do?

It turns the BOQ from a tender document into a live budget. Every site entry is matched to the BOQ: a measurement, a GRN, a labour attendance sheet, a petty-cash slip.

A working setup answers four questions at any time:

- **How much of each BOQ item is executed**, and how much is left?
- **How much have we billed** against it, and is anything executed but unbilled?
- **What has the work actually cost** in material, labour, equipment and site expenses?
- **Where is the gap** between planned and actual, and is it growing?

The goal is simple. The research behind this page calls it real-time visibility into the variance between the budgeted BOQ and actual site consumption, *before* overruns compound [ref 22].

## Two variances most contractors mix up

Most "BOQ vs actual" reports blend two different problems into one number. Splitting them tells you whether you have a billing problem or a cost problem, and they need different fixes.

### Quantity variance: are we executing and billing what the BOQ says?

This is the revenue side. It compares BOQ quantity with executed quantity and billed quantity for each line item.

It catches **over-billing** (claiming more than the BOQ allows, which the client's QS will disallow) and **unbilled work** (work that was done but got lost between the [measurement book](/solutions/measurement-book-software/) and the bill). It also catches **deviations and extra items** that weren't in the original BOQ.

### Cost variance: is the work costing what we priced it at?

This is the margin side. It compares the budgeted cost of the work with the actual cost of material issued, labour deployed, equipment hours and site expenses.

You can bill a line item perfectly and still lose money on it, for example through steel wastage, idle labour or a rate that was too thin at tender. Only cost variance shows that.

> **Key takeaway:** if billed value is on track but margin is shrinking, you have a cost variance. If cost is on track but cash isn't coming in, you have a quantity or billing variance. Good BOQ vs actual software shows both.

## Why spreadsheets and WhatsApp can't give you BOQ vs actual

On most Indian sites, the data for BOQ vs actual already exists. It's scattered: progress photos on [WhatsApp](/solutions/why-whatsapp-fails-for-construction/), BOQ tracking in Excel, and RA bills in separate accounting software [ref 6]. Nobody can add those up in real time.

It isn't a lack of tools. Indian construction firms lead Asia-Pacific in digital adoption and use an average of **7.5 digital tools per firm**, according to the 2024 Autodesk and Deloitte *State of Digital Adoption in Construction* report [ref 4]. The trouble is that those tools don't talk to each other, so the BOQ and the actuals never meet.

The cost of that gap is visible at national scale. MoSPI has reported that **over 450 central infrastructure projects** carry cumulative cost overruns of more than **₹4.28 lakh crore**. The causes it lists include execution delays, fragmented site-to-office communication and weak project monitoring [ref 1]. A BOQ vs actual view is a project-level answer to that monitoring gap.

Actual cost builds up from the entries your team already makes:

- **Equipment:** machine hours and fuel

Material issued, labour marked and equipment hours become project cost automatically. The app is [offline-first](/solutions/offline-construction-app/), so entries made in a basement or on a remote highway stretch sync when the signal returns.

### 3. Measure and bill against the BOQ

Site measurements record executed quantity against BOQ lines and reduce the balance. Executed quantity flows into abstracts and [RA bills](/solutions/ra-bill-software/) at the BOQ rate, so nobody re-keys rates and billing can't run past the BOQ unnoticed. Work beyond the BOQ is flagged as an extra or deviation item.

## Who uses BOQ vs actual cost tracking?

**CFO / finance head.** Wants project cost control without waiting for month-end. They see budget vs actual and project P&L across sites, and get an early warning when a job's margin starts to slip.

**Commercial manager / QS.** Owns the BOQ and the client relationship. They track deviations and extra items as they happen, so variations are claimed in time instead of argued about at final bill.

**Billing engineer.** Raises RA bills. They see exactly how much of each item is left before the next bill, so claims never exceed the BOQ and get approved the first time.

**Project manager.** Runs the site. They see which items are eating budget faster than they progress, and can act on wastage, idle labour or rework that week.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between BOQ and actual cost?
The BOQ is the planned quantity and rate for each item of work, agreed at tender or contract stage. Actual cost is what the site really spends to execute that work on material, labour, equipment and site expenses. The gap between the two is your cost variance, and it decides whether the project makes money.

### How do you track BOQ vs actual cost in construction?
Link every site entry (measurements, GRNs, labour attendance, machine hours and petty cash) to the project and its BOQ. Compare executed and billed quantity with BOQ quantity, and compare actual cost with the budgeted cost. Software does this as entries come in. Excel needs someone to re-key and reconcile it by hand.

### Can BOQ vs actual cost software prevent cost overruns?
It can't stop a price rise, but it shows the overrun early enough to act: renegotiate a rate, control wastage, raise a variation or re-plan labour. Overruns get expensive when they're discovered at final reconciliation, after the budget is gone. That's the problem MoSPI's ₹4.28 lakh crore overrun figure points to at national scale [ref 1].

### Does BOQ vs actual software work with RA billing?
It should. In True Site Sync, measurements draw down executed quantity from the BOQ and flow into abstracts and RA bills at the BOQ rate. The billed quantity and the balance always match, and you can't bill an item past its BOQ quantity without seeing it.

### Can I import my existing BOQ from Excel?
Yes. You can upload a BOQ from an Excel template into a project, and its items and rates are immediately available for measurement, billing and cost tracking.

### Does it integrate with Tally?
True Site Sync does not currently offer a direct Tally integration. It exports reports to PDF and Excel, and many contractors keep Tally for statutory accounting while running site execution, BOQ billing and job costing in True Site Sync.
