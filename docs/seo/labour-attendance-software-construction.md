# Labour Attendance Software for Construction Sites: From Muster Roll to Wages in One Record

If your wage bill is built from a muster roll someone fills in at the end of the day, you're paying for the register, not the site. **Labour attendance software for construction sites** records who is actually working, at the site, on the day, and carries that record into wages and project cost.

> **What is labour attendance software for construction?**
> Labour attendance software lets site supervisors mark attendance for workers, staff and gangs on a phone at the site, including where there's no network. Each entry is dated and tied to the project. Overtime, advances and wages are calculated from it, and labour cost lands on the project automatically.

## What does labour attendance software for construction sites do?

It replaces three registers that rarely agree: the muster roll, the wage sheet and the cost report. A good system does four jobs:

- **Records presence where the work happens.** Attendance is marked on site, not reconstructed later in the office.
- **Handles every kind of worker.** Daily-wage labour, monthly staff and gangs brought in by a labour contractor.
- **Calculates what's owed.** Overtime, advances paid during the week and the net wage due.
- **Sends labour cost to the right project**, so the owner sees what each site's workforce actually costs.

## Where paper muster rolls leak money

A paper muster roll can be filled in by anyone, at any time, from anywhere. That's where the leaks start.

- **Ghost and proxy attendance.** Names that weren't on site still get paid. One worker marks for an absent friend.
- **End-of-day reconstruction.** When attendance is written up from memory, disputes follow, and the supervisor's word is all you have.
- **Advances that go missing.** Cash advances given mid-week are noted on scraps of paper and forgotten at settlement.
- **No link to cost.** The wage total is known, but not which project or activity it belongs to.

Our [construction labour attendance and wage management guide](/blog/construction-labour-attendance-wage-management-india/) covers the muster roll, wage register and settlement process in detail. This page is about the tool.

## Built for the people who actually mark attendance

The person who buys attendance software is rarely the one who uses it. Procurement heads search in English. The supervisors, storekeepers and site engineers who generate the data mostly work, and search, in Hindi, Hinglish or a regional language [ref 44]. They type things like *site par hajiri lagane wala app* (an app to mark attendance on site) or *mistri aur labour attendance app* (a mason and labour attendance app).

Software that only works for the office won't survive on site. That's why the daily actions in True Site Sync are kept to about two taps, work without signal, and stay light on battery and data.

## What to check before you choose a construction attendance app

- **Works offline.** Test it in the worst-signal corner of your site, not in the office.
- **Covers gangs, not just individuals.** Most sites run both.
- **Each person marks only their own entries.** This is a basic control against proxy attendance.
- **Overtime and advances in the same place** as attendance.
- **Feeds wages and project cost** without exporting to Excel.
- **Exports for records.** You'll still need registers for audits and labour inspections. Check the reports can be exported as PDF or Excel.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is the best app for labour attendance on a construction site?
Pick the one your supervisors will actually use every day. It should work without network, take seconds per entry, cover individual workers and gangs, and feed wages and project cost automatically. Run a free trial on your worst-signal site before you decide.

### Can construction attendance be marked without internet?
Yes, if the app is offline-first. In True Site Sync, attendance saves on the phone instantly and syncs to the office when the phone gets a connection, so nothing is lost on sites with no signal.

### How does attendance software stop ghost or proxy attendance?
It records attendance on site, on the day, with a dated entry the owner can review, and each person marks only their own entries. Our [labour attendance guide](/blog/construction-labour-attendance-wage-management-india/) explains how fictitious entries usually get onto paper registers.

### Can it calculate labour wages and overtime?
Yes. Overtime, advances and payroll are recorded against the same attendance, so the wage due comes from one record instead of being re-added from the register.

### Does it work for labour contractors and gangs?
Yes. You can mark attendance for gang teams as well as individual workers, and labour contractors can convert measured work into bills, so piece-rate and daily-wage work are both covered.

### Does labour attendance connect to project cost?
Yes. Labour marked on site becomes project cost automatically and rolls into the project's cost abstract and profit, next to material and equipment.
