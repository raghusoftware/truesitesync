# Construction Software Pricing in India: What You'll Actually Pay in 2026

Construction software quotes in India range from a few thousand rupees a year to enterprise contracts that need a procurement committee. This **construction software pricing India** guide explains how pricing works, which costs sit outside the headline price, and what published plans actually cost, so you can compare quotes on the same basis.

> **How much does construction software cost in India?**
> Construction software in India is priced per user, per tier of users, per project, or by custom enterprise quote. Published annual plans start from about ₹2,500 a year for a single user. For true comparison, add implementation, training, data migration and GST to the subscription. The total cost of ownership is the number that matters.

## Construction software pricing models in India: the four you'll be quoted

- **Per user, per month or year.** You pay for each named login. This is simple to understand, but costs climb fast when you add supervisors and storekeepers, and those field users are exactly who needs the app.
- **Tiered by team size.** One annual price covers up to N users. It's predictable, and you're not penalised for adding a site engineer until you cross the tier.
- **Per project or per site.** You pay for each active project. This suits firms with few, large projects but gets expensive as site count grows.
- **Custom enterprise quote.** This is negotiated, usually bundled with implementation, integrations and support terms. It's common for large EPC and real-estate firms that need security reviews and custom workflows [Strategic Conversion Methodologies].

Ask every vendor which model they use. Then convert all quotes to **annual cost for your actual headcount**, including field users.

## What drives the price up (or down)

- **Who needs a login.** If site supervisors, storekeepers and billing engineers all need access, field users usually outnumber office users. Per-user pricing hits hardest here.
- **Which modules are included.** Some vendors include everything. Others gate modules such as billing, labour or multi-site behind higher plans. Check the plan sheet line by line.
- **India-specific workflows.** RA billing, measurement book, GST invoicing, retention and TDS are standard needs for Indian contractors. Global tools may need customisation, or may not support them at all.
- **Offline capability.** Many Indian sites have unstable connectivity [ref 9]. An app that needs constant signal costs you in lost entries and poor adoption, even if its licence is cheaper.
- **Deployment and support level.** Dedicated onboarding, account managers and SLAs add cost, but can be worth it for large rollouts.

## Hidden costs to ask about before you sign

Get these in writing:

1. **Implementation or setup fee.** Is project setup and BOQ import done for you, and is it charged separately?
2. **Training.** Is on-site or online training included, and how many sessions?
3. **Data migration.** Will they import your existing BOQs, vendor lists and open bills?
4. **Extra users mid-year.** What does one more site engineer cost in month 7?
5. **Module upgrades.** If you need billing or labour later, does that mean a plan jump?
6. **Storage and photos.** Is there a cap on photos and documents?
7. **Integrations and API.** Is accounting export or API access extra?
8. **GST.** Is the quote inclusive or exclusive of GST?
9. **Renewal price.** Is the first-year price a discount that disappears at renewal?
10. **Exit.** Can you export all your data, in a usable format, if you leave?

## How to estimate your total cost

Use this simple **total cost of ownership (TCO)** for year one:

> **Year-one TCO** = annual subscription for your real headcount (office + field)
> + implementation and data migration
> + training
> + paid add-ons (modules, integrations, storage)
> + GST

Do the same for year two without the one-time items. A tool that costs more upfront but covers RA billing, measurement and offline use out of the box often has a lower TCO than a cheaper licence that needs custom work.

## Is free construction software worth it?

"Free construction software download" gets large search volume, but the research behind this page found it's mostly people looking for cracked or open-source tools, students and template hunters [ref Negative Keywords]. For a business, "free" usually means one of three things:

- **A free trial.** Fine. It's the best way to test a tool on your own site.
- **A limited free tier.** Useful for one person, but check what happens to your data when you outgrow it.
- **Unlicensed or cracked software.** This risks your project data and your firm's reputation, with no support when billing day goes wrong.

Excel is free too, but it's not where your margin leaks get caught. See [Excel alternatives for construction management](/solutions/excel-alternative-for-construction-management/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does construction management software cost in India?
Published annual plans start from about ₹2,500 a year for one user, and team plans scale up from there. True Site Sync, for example, runs from ₹5,000 for up to 5 users to ₹50,000 for up to 50. Enterprise deployments with custom integrations are usually quoted separately. Always compare total cost of ownership, not just the licence.

### Is construction software priced per user or per project?
Both models exist, along with tiered plans and custom enterprise quotes. Per-user pricing gets expensive when field staff need logins. Tiered plans give a fixed annual cost up to a user limit. Ask each vendor to quote for your actual headcount, including site users.

### What hidden costs should I watch for?
Implementation and setup, training, data migration, extra users added mid-year, module upgrades, storage caps, integration or API fees, GST, and renewal price increases. Ask for each one in writing before you sign.

### Is there a free trial before I pay?
Many vendors offer one. True Site Sync offers a 6-day unrestricted trial with no credit card, so you can test it on a live site before committing.

### Is GST charged on construction software subscriptions?
Yes, GST applies on top of the subscription price. Published prices are usually exclusive of GST, so confirm whether a quote includes it. [VERIFY with CA: applicable GST rate, if you choose to state it.]

### Is cheaper construction software good enough for a small contractor?
It can be, if it covers the workflows you actually run: measurement, RA billing, material and labour, and it works offline on your sites. A cheap tool that your site team won't use costs more than a slightly dearer one they will.
