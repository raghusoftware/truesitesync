Billing engineer in construction: role, skills and how to start

Billing engineers turn site work into money. They measure what was built, record it in the measurement book, and prepare the RA and final bills that get the contractor paid. Here's what the job involves and how a civil engineering student or fresher can practise it.

In one line. A billing engineer measures executed work as per IS 1200 and the contract, records it in the measurement book (MB), and prepares, checks and submits RA bills, final bills, reconciliations and claims. They work alongside the site engineer and the client's engineer.

Roles and responsibilities

Measurement

Joint site measurement with the client's engineer, and MB entries backed by drawings.

RA bills

Monthly running bills: up-to-date quantity, minus previous, times rate, plus GST, minus deductions. See how to prepare an RA bill.

Subcontractor bills

Certifying what subcontractors and labour contractors are owed. See subcontractor billing.

Reconciliation

Material reconciliation of cement and steel, and BOQ vs executed quantities.

Extra items and claims

Deviations, extra items, price escalation and delay claims.

Final bill

Closing the contract: final quantities, retention release and recoveries.

A 30-day practice plan for students and freshers

Week 1

Take one G+1 house drawing and work out footing, column and slab concrete quantities by hand.

Week 2

Prepare the BBS for the slab and the brickwork quantities with deductions.

Week 3

Write the MB entries and an abstract for the work "done" up to plinth.

Week 4

Make RA bill 1 and RA bill 2 with previous quantities, GST, TDS and retention.

Mistakes new billing engineers make

  • Measuring from the drawing instead of the site, or the other way round, without a joint record.
  • Forgetting to carry forward previous quantities, so work gets billed twice or missed.
  • Billing extra items without written approval.
  • Mixing GST and TDS bases.
  • Not reconciling cement and steel until the final bill.

Frequently asked questions

What does a billing engineer do in construction?

Measures executed work, records it in the measurement book, and prepares and checks RA bills, final bills, subcontractor bills, reconciliations and claims.

What qualification is needed to become a billing engineer?

A diploma or degree in civil engineering, plus practical skill in drawings, IS 1200 measurement, BOQ, the measurement book, RA billing and Excel.

What is IS 1200?

The Bureau of Indian Standards series that sets out how to measure building and civil engineering works, including units and deductions.

Is billing engineer a good career?

Yes. Every contractor, builder and PMC needs billing engineers, because billing is how projects get paid. It also leads into quantity surveying, contracts and project management roles.

How can a fresher get billing experience?

Practise on a real drawing set: work out the quantities, write MB entries, and prepare two RA bills with deductions. A construction billing app's free trial lets you produce real RA bill PDFs to show employers.

Billing engineer ka kaam kya hota hai?

Site par hua kaam naapna, MB me entry karna, aur RA bill, final bill, subcontractor bill aur material reconciliation banana: yani kaam ko paise me badalna.
