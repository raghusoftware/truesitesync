Most construction firms that think they need custom software actually need cleaner processes. Fix how measurement, billing and purchasing flow first, buy a configurable tool for the standard 80%, and only build what is truly unique to you.

By the True Site Sync team · Last updated 28 October 2026

Short answer. Up to a few hundred crore of turnover, off-the-shelf construction software almost always wins on cost, speed and upkeep. At very large scale, some customisation is inevitable. Even then, the bigger gain comes from using the rollout to fix broken processes instead of copying them into code.

Why "we need custom" is usually a process problem

Companies grow faster than their systems. Each site invents its own measurement sheet, each accountant has a favourite Excel, and approvals live in someone's head. When a standard tool "doesn't fit", it's often because there is no single process for it to fit. Write down how a measurement becomes a bill and a GRN becomes a payment before you evaluate any software.

👤

📱

Mobile and offline

⏳

Time to value

Months of building before the first site uses it, against days for a ready tool.

Checklist before you build anything

1. Map the money flow

BOQ → measurement → RA bill → payment, and indent → PO → GRN → bill → payment.

2. Mark what's truly unique

Usually under 20% of the flow.

3. Trial two ready tools

Run one live site for a full billing cycle.

4. Check the exits

Excel and PDF exports, and a Tally export for your CA, so no tool locks your data in.

5. Build only the gap

As a report or an integration, not a second system.

Frequently asked questions

Does a construction company need custom software?

Usually not. Small and mid-size contractors are better served by ready construction software that is configured, not coded. Very large firms may need some customisation, but the bigger gain is fixing processes during the rollout.

Is custom ERP cheaper than off-the-shelf in the long run?

Rarely for construction firms. Custom software carries ongoing maintenance, compliance updates and key-person risk that subscription software spreads across many customers.

When does custom construction software make sense?

When a workflow is genuinely unique to your business and no configurable tool can handle it. Even then, buy the core and build only a small extension or integration.

What should I check before buying construction software?

Native RA billing and measurement, GST and TDS, offline use on site, roles and approvals, multi-site views, data export, the total yearly cost and a trial on a live site.
