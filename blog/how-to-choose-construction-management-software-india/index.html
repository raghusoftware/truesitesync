The 10-point checklist

Most software fails Indian contractors for the same handful of reasons — it's foreign to local billing, too heavy for the field, or hides its real cost behind implementation fees. Score any tool you're considering against these ten, in order of how often they decide success:

1. Works offline

Remote sites have no signal. If capture stalls without internet, the field won't use it. See offline app.

2. Native RA & BOQ billing

Running-account bills against a BOQ from a measurement book — not generic invoices.

3. Retention, TDS & GST

Must handle retention, Section 194C TDS and CGST/SGST/IGST automatically.

4. Field-simple (two-tap test)

Daily logs in about two taps, or crews fall back to WhatsApp and paper.

5. Connected end to end

Measurement → bill → cost → profit, so numbers reconcile without re-keying.

6. Honest price

Watch for implementation fees. Prefer a free trial and transparent annual pricing.

7. Multi-project & roles

Project-scoped access so each person sees only their sites.

8. Exports

Everything to PDF and Excel — your data, when you want it.

9. Data security & portability

Cloud backup, and no lock-in on your own records.

10. Try before you buy

A real free trial on the full product, no card required.

Why offline and native billing matter most

If you weight only two of the ten, weight offline capability and native RA/BOQ billing. Everything else can be worked around; these two cannot. Software that can't be used at the workface simply doesn't get used, and software that can't raise a compliant RA bill leaves you back in the spreadsheet you were trying to escape. Global platforms like Procore are strong on breadth but weak on both for Indian contractors — see True Site Sync vs Procore and Procore vs Zoho for the detail.

Beware the implementation trap

The sticker price is rarely the real price. Enterprise platforms often add onboarding and implementation fees that can run into lakhs, plus per-module upsells for features you actually need (financials, scheduling).
