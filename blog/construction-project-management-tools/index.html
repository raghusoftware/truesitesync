Frequently asked questions

What is the best project management tool for construction?

For site teams, a construction app that ties progress to quantities, labour, material and billing. For master scheduling on large projects, add MS Project or Primavera P6. General task boards suit office to-dos.

Is there a free project management tool for construction?

Yes. ProjectLibre is a free open-source scheduler, and task boards such as Trello and Zoho Projects have free tiers. Construction apps usually offer free trials. True Site Sync has a 6-day trial.

Is MS Project or Primavera better for construction?

Primavera P6 is standard on large infrastructure and EPC jobs where clients require it. MS Project is simpler and common on building projects. Neither records daily site data or billing.

Can Trello or Asana be used for construction?

For office tasks and design coordination, yes. They don't handle BOQs, measurements, wages or GST bills, so site money stays in Excel.
